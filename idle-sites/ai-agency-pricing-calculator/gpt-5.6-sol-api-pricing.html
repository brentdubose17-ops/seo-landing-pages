GPT-5.6 Sol API Pricing: OpenAI Cuts Rates to $4/$20 (Aug 21 – Nov 21, 2026)

Published August 22, 2026By ABD Legacy LLC
GPT-5.6 Sol API pricing OpenAI pricing AI model cost

What happened. On Aug 21, 2026, OpenAI cut API and credit pricing for its flagship top-tier model, GPT-5.6 Sol, by over 20% for the next three months — the first price cut on Sol since launch. New rates: $4 per 1M input tokens and $20 per 1M output tokens, with cached input at $0.40 per 1M (down from $5/$30 and $0.50).

The window

The promotional pricing is available Aug 21 – Nov 21, 2026 (at least through November 21). It applies to the API and eligible ChatGPT Work and Codex credits; ChatGPT Pro, Plus, and Business subscription usage is unchanged.

What it means for agency cost math

Input-heavy agent workloads drop roughly 20%+ at the new rate — a 10K-in/2K-out Sol task falls from ~$0.110 to ~$0.080, and a 100K-in/5K-out run from ~$0.650 to ~$0.500. Sol remains the premium frontier tier, so routing guidance is unchanged; the cut narrows the premium gap to open-weight and mid-tier models.

Frequently asked questions

What is GPT-5.6 Sol API pricing after the cut?

GPT-5.6 Sol now costs $4 per 1M input tokens and $20 per 1M output tokens, with cached input at $0.40 per 1M — down from $5/$30 and $0.50. The promotional rate is valid Aug 21 through at least Nov 21, 2026, per OpenAI's Aug 21, 2026 announcement.

How long does the GPT-5.6 Sol promotional pricing last?

The GPT-5.6 Sol promotional pricing is available at least through November 21, 2026 (the three-month window from the Aug 21, 2026 announcement). After the window, pricing is expected — but not guaranteed — to revert to $5/$30.

Does the GPT-5.6 Sol price cut apply to ChatGPT subscriptions?

No. The cut applies to the API and to eligible ChatGPT Work and Codex credits. ChatGPT Pro, Plus, and Business subscription usage remains unchanged.
