ChatGPT Business Premium Seats: Pricing, Usage Limits & Comparison (2026)

Published August 25, 2026Updated August 25, 2026By ABD Legacy LLC

ChatGPT Business Premium Seats cost $125 per user per month billed monthly, or $100 per user per month billed annually. Standard seats remain $25 per user per month, or $20 per user per month billed annually. Premium seats include 5x more usage than Standard seats and are not subject to the five-hour usage limit — announced August 2026.

OpenAI introduced Premium seats for ChatGPT Business in August 2026: a higher-usage seat tier layered on top of the existing Standard seat, pitched at small businesses and startups whose teams keep hitting ChatGPT's limits. If you're budgeting an AI tool stack per seat — for your own team or for client deployments — this changes the math.

What changed: Premium seats on ChatGPT Business

Until now, ChatGPT Business (the plan formerly called ChatGPT Team, renamed on August 29, 2025) had one seat type. Premium seats add a second tier so workspace owners can give power users more capacity without moving the whole team to Enterprise. The key upgrades, per OpenAI's announcement:

  • 5x more usage than Standard seats — enough for bigger projects and fewer interruptions.
  • No five-hour usage limit — the rolling cap that throttles heavy users is removed for Premium seats.
  • Predictable weekly usage resets instead of the standard cadence.
  • Same included apps — ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex — just with a higher usage allotment.

There is no fixed credit or dollar equivalent for Premium's included usage — consumption depends on model and task, and included usage is separate from the workspace credit pool.

Billing mechanics to budget around:

  • Adding seats triggers an immediate prorated charge for the rest of the billing cycle (OpenAI's worked example: adding 3 Standard + 1 Premium on day 10 of a $875/mo plan costs $133.33 immediately).
  • Reducing seats takes effect at the next billing cycle — you are charged for all paid seats whether or not they're assigned, and seats are non-refundable once committed.
  • Billing cadence is per-subscription: all seats share monthly or annual billing; changing cadence takes effect at renewal.

The 5-hour usage limit change

Standard ChatGPT Business seats are subject to the rolling five-hour usage limit, which throttles heavy users mid-task. Premium seats remove that limit entirely — the headline upgrade for teams that run long sessions in Codex or ChatGPT Work. If your team's work is bursts of deep usage rather than steady light usage, Premium is the seat tier that fixes the actual pain point.

Who should buy Premium seats

  • Small businesses and startups — the segment OpenAI explicitly targets: lean teams doing operating plans, marketing campaigns, and product work in ChatGPT daily.
  • Teams with 1–5 heavy users — mix Premium for the power users and Standard for everyone else; the 2-seat minimum and 200-seat cap are easy to satisfy.
  • Agencies — model per-seat AI costs for your own tool stack or pass the comparison on to clients building AI into their operations. It's a real line item once usage is heavy.
  • Not for: >200-seat deployments (that's Enterprise), orgs needing invoice billing or BAAs (Enterprise only), or teams that only need API access (API is billed separately and not included).

What about the launch promotion?

OpenAI ran a limited launch offer: $100 in workspace credits per qualifying Premium seat, up to 5 seats ($500), for the first 10,000 eligible workspaces. The waitlist signup closed August 20, 2026 — as of late August 2026 the promo window is no longer open to new signups, so price Premium seats at list unless you already hold a qualifying seat-add window. OpenAI does not train on ChatGPT Business workspace data, and API usage remains billed separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much do ChatGPT Business Premium Seats cost?

A: ChatGPT Business Premium Seats cost $125 per user per month billed monthly, or $100 per user per month billed annually (a 20% annual discount). Standard seats remain $25 per user per month, or $20 per user per month billed annually. A workspace requires at least 2 paid seats in any combination of Standard and Premium.

Q: How much does ChatGPT Business cost?

A: ChatGPT Business costs $25 per user per month for Standard seats, or $20 per user per month billed annually. The new Premium seats cost $125 per user per month, or $100 per user per month billed annually. Prices are in USD and may vary by country and currency; a workspace requires at least 2 paid seats and is capped at 200 paid seats per subscription since August 24, 2026.

Q: What is the difference between ChatGPT Business Standard and Premium seats?

A: Premium seats include 5x more usage than Standard seats, are not subject to the five-hour usage limit, and get predictable weekly usage resets. Both seat types include ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex access. You can mix Standard and Premium seats in the same workspace and upgrade, downgrade, reassign, or swap seat types as needs change.

Q: Can I mix Standard and Premium seats in ChatGPT Business?

A: Yes. A ChatGPT Business workspace can mix Standard and Premium seats under one subscription — for example, 1 Standard and 1 Premium satisfies the 2-seat minimum. Only workspace owners can purchase, add, reduce, assign, reassign, or change seat types.

Q: Is ChatGPT Team the same as ChatGPT Business?

A: Yes. ChatGPT Team was renamed to ChatGPT Business on August 29, 2025 — it is the same plan. ChatGPT Enterprise is the separate, sales-led tier for larger deployments and more advanced controls, including invoice billing and custom contracts.

Bottom line for 2026

Premium seats give ChatGPT Business a real two-tier structure: $100/user/month annual (or $125 monthly) for power users who need 5x usage and no five-hour cap, layered on the $20–$25 Standard seat. For small businesses and startups, the practical move is mixing a few Premium seats for heavy users and keeping the rest on Standard — the 20% annual discount and the 2-seat minimum make the annual plan the default to model.
