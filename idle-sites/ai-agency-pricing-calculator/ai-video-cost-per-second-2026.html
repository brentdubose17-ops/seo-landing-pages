How the math works

Every major video API bills per second of output video, by resolution. The total is a single multiplication: duration (seconds) × per-second price (tier). That is the whole formula — the tiers are what differ.

Cost per video = durationSeconds × pricePerSecond(tier) Example — Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash, 360p draft tier ($0.03/s): 30s → 30 × $0.03 = $0.90 60s → 60 × $0.03 = $1.80 90s → 90 × $0.03 = $2.70 Example — Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash, standard 720p ($0.10/s): 30s → 30 × $0.10 = $3.00 60s → 60 × $0.10 = $6.00 90s → 90 × $0.10 = $9.00 Example — fal MiniMax H3 Max, 768p list ($0.08/s): 60s → 60 × $0.08 = $4.80/min (matches fal's own listing)

The Google Cloud pricing page confirms the Omni ladder with token math: 1,931 tokens/second at 360p, 5,792 at 720p, 8,688 at 1080p, 17,376 at 4K, billed at $17.50 per 1M video-output tokens — which works out to ≈$0.034/s, ≈$0.101/s, ≈$0.152/s and ≈$0.304/s respectively, rounded to the published $0.03/$0.10/$0.15/$0.30 ladder.

Why Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash resets the per-second floor

Google GA'd Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash on Aug 27, 2026 — the stable successor to the Gemini Omni Flash preview (June 30, 2026) — available via the Gemini API in Google AI Studio, the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, Google Flow (AI Plus/Pro/Ultra), and scene extension in the Gemini app. Its 360p draft tier at $0.03/s (~60% faster and roughly one-third the cost of its own 720p output) is the cheapest listed per-second rate among major vendors; its $0.10/s 720p tier matches Sora 2, Veo 3.1 Fast and the previous Omni Flash.

What you get at that price: 40-second scenes built from 10-second extensions, first/last frame control, up to 3 seconds of video reference, 10 seconds of lookback context (up from 1s), and native synchronized audio. 1080p and 4K are upscaled outputs, not native renders — the standard generation tier is 720p. For agencies, the practical takeaway: a usable 30-second draft costs under a dollar, and the same clip at client-ready 720p costs $3.00 in raw API spend.

Watch-outs: Sora 2's API shuts down September 24, 2026 — any quote built on Sora per-second math has a hard expiry. And fal MiniMax H3 Max's attention-grabbing $0.04/s is a 14-day intro; the list price is $0.08/s at 768p.

Frequently asked questions

How much does AI video cost per second in 2026?

There is no single number — it depends on model and resolution. Google's Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash is the new floor at $0.03 per second for 360p draft output, $0.10/s at 720p, $0.15/s at 1080p and $0.30/s at 4K. Competitors run $0.05–$0.70/s: Runway Gen-4 Turbo $0.05/s, Veo 3.1 Lite $0.05/s, fal MiniMax H3 Max $0.08/s (768p list, $0.04/s intro), Sora 2 $0.10/s, Runway Gen-4.5 $0.12/s, Kling $0.084–$0.42/s, Sora 2 Pro $0.30–$0.70/s.

What is the cheapest AI video generator per second in 2026?

Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash (Google, GA Aug 27, 2026) has the cheapest listed per-second API rate among major vendors: $0.03 per second for its 360p draft tier — roughly one-third the cost of its own standard 720p output. Runway Gen-4 Turbo ($0.05/s) and Veo 3.1 Lite ($0.05/s) are next; fal MiniMax H3 Max lists $0.08/s at 768p with a 14-day $0.04/s intro and a free tier of 5 clips/day.

How much does Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash cost per second?

Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash bills per second of output video by resolution: $0.03/s at 360p (draft mode), $0.10/s at 720p (standard), $0.15/s at 1080p and $0.30/s at 4K. 1080p and 4K are upscaled from 720p, not native renders. A 10-second 360p draft costs about $0.30; a 10-second 1080p clip costs about $1.50.

How does Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash compare to Sora and Runway on price?

Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash starts at $0.03/s (360p draft) and matches Sora 2 at $0.10/s for 720p, with 1080p at $0.15/s and 4K at $0.30/s. Sora 2 is $0.10/s at 720p and its API sunsets September 24, 2026; Sora 2 Pro is $0.30–$0.70/s. Runway Gen-4 Turbo is $0.05/s and Gen-4.5 is $0.12/s (both credit-based, $0.01/credit). Omni adds 40-second scenes, keyframe control and native audio that per-second video SKUs from Veo don't include.

What does a 30/60/90-second AI video cost with Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash?
