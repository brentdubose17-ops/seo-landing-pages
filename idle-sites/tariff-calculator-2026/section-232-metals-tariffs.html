Section 232 metals tariffs: what is in force and what is still pending

Published September 11, 2026 · Updated September 11, 2026By ABD Legacy LLC

As of September 11, 2026, Section 232 duties are in force on steel and aluminium articles, on semi-finished copper products and copper-intensive derivatives, and on drones and polysilicon derivatives — but no refined-copper tariff exists. The White House decision on refined copper is still pending, so nothing is collectible on cathode, anode or concentrate.

The one open metals item: refined copper

Refined copper is the only open file in this table. Reuters reported on September 10, 2026 that the White House had not decided whether to impose a refined-copper duty, and since then no proclamation, Federal Register notice or Commerce determination has created one — so nothing is collectible on cathode, anode or concentrate.

The market moved on the expectation anyway. LME copper set an all-time high of $14,875/t during the September 10 session, then fell 3.1% to $14,312/t, and COMEX futures dropped more than 4% intraday. On 20 tonnes at $14,230/t the dutiable value is $284,600; at the recommended — not adopted — 15% rate, landed cost moves to $327,290, up $42,690 or 15.0%. At the $14,875/t peak the same 15% rate would have cost $44,625 instead of $42,690, with no change in the rate at all: the duty base moves with the market.

Section 232 FAQ: the two questions importers ask

Is there a Section 232 duty on refined copper right now?

No. As of September 11, 2026 the White House has not decided and no Federal Register instrument imposes a refined-copper duty, so nothing is collectible on cathode, anode or concentrate. The in-force Section 232 copper regime covers semi-finished products and derivatives only.

Which Section 232 rates does the calculator actually apply?

The calculator applies the pending refined-copper scenario you set (0-30%) and the Section 232 categories it already models, such as drones/UAS and polysilicon. It does not add Section 232 steel or aluminium duties to a steel estimate, so that number is a lower bound.
