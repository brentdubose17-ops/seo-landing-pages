Claude Code limits drop 17% on Sept 14 — what agencies should do

Published August 30, 2026Updated September 14, 2026By ABD Legacy LLC
Claude Code AI coding agents Agency operations Cost planning

The short version: Anthropic's temporary +50% Claude Code weekly-limit boost ended September 13, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT; from September 14, 2026 standard weekly limits are permanently 25% higher than the pre-promotion baseline for Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans — which is about 17% below the promotional level, Anthropic's own figure. Normalize the pre-promotion baseline to 100 and the sequence is 100 → 150 (promotion) → 125 (permanent), so 125 ÷ 100 = +25% and 125 ÷ 150 = −16.7%. For agencies running Claude Code, the net effect versus the promotion is a 17% reduction in weekly capacity — the company confirmed the number itself: "Compared to today, this works out to a 17% reduction in weekly limits on Claude Code." If your delivery pipeline ran near the cap you had in August, your throughput ceiling is lower today and the cost-per-task math needs a re-run.

What changed: the +50% boost expired and a +25% baseline took over

Anthropic announced on X (via @ClaudeDevs, August 29, 2026) that it was permanently raising Claude Code's standard weekly usage limits by 25% for Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans. The catch: the temporary 50% weekly boost — live since May 13, 2026 and extended through the summer — ended first, at 11:59 PM PT on September 13, 2026, one day before the new level took effect. Both dates now sit on Anthropic's own surface: its Help Center article "Claude Code May–August 2026 weekly limits promotion" carries a 2026-09-14 update stamp, states that the promotion "ran from May 13 through September 13, 2026", and states that the permanent level took effect "starting September 14, 2026" — see the update note below for the verbatim text.

"Starting September 14, we're permanently raising standard weekly limits in Claude Code by 25% for Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans. Until then, the current 50% increase will be in place." — Anthropic via @ClaudeDevs, August 29, 2026 (the pre-change announcement, quoted verbatim)

Because the permanent level replaced the larger temporary boost, paid users lost capacity. The canonical illustration, used by BleepingComputer, MacObserver, and others:

PeriodWeekly limit (relative to pre-promotion baseline)
Pre-promotion baseline (before May 13, 2026)100%
Promotion — May 13 through Sept 13, 2026 (temporary +50% boost, now ended)150%
From Sept 14, 2026 (permanent — the level in force today)125%

Going from 150 to 125 is a 16.7% cut — 25 ÷ 150 = 16.7%, which Anthropic and every major outlet round to about 17%. Read against the old baseline instead and the same 125 is a 25% increase (125 ÷ 100 = 1.25): both headlines describe one limit and differ only in the denominator. Returning to the promotional level would take a further +20% (25 ÷ 125).

Why it matters: agency throughput and cost-per-task

Claude Code is a core delivery tool for many agencies: automations, client sites, internal tooling. A weekly limit is not a soft suggestion — it is the ceiling on how much agent work your seats can produce in a week. Two effects follow:

  • Throughput ceiling is lower. A workload that consumed 100% of the promotional cap now consumes 120% of the standard cap (150 ÷ 125 = 1.2). The same weekly workload needs 20% more plan headroom or usage credits than it did during the promotion.
  • Cost-per-task moved in the wrong direction. If you priced client work at the promotional capacity, the same task volume now needs more seats, an upgrade, or a queue. Fixed-fee retainers priced on boosted throughput absorb the difference unless you adjust them.

What did not change: Anthropic's promotion page states the boost "did not affect" five-hour session limits, and no five-hour change has been announced since; the permanent sentence covers weekly limits only, and no plan-price or API-billing change was announced. This is a weekly-capacity change only. The permanent sentence names Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise plans and does not restate the promotion's exclusions, so no claim is made here about Free plans or consumption-based Enterprise seats either way.

How many Claude Code requests per week do you get?

The honest answer: Anthropic does not publish weekly Claude Code limits as fixed message or token counts. Usage varies by model, conversation length, tool usage, and effort level, which is why the company communicates limits as ratios rather than numbers. What you can verify for your own account: on Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise, open Settings > Usage to see progress bars for your five-hour and weekly limits. That is the only reliable way to know how close you are running to the ceiling.

Claude Code usage limits 2026: what changed and what didn't

  • Weekly limits: changed. 150% of baseline during the promotion → 125% since Sept 14, 2026 for Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise. Net −17% versus the promotional level, +25% versus the pre-promotion baseline.
  • Five-hour session limits: not affected by the promotion. Anthropic's promotion page says the boost did not affect them, and no five-hour change has been announced since.
  • Plan pricing: unchanged. No price change was announced.
  • API / consumption billing: unchanged. The announcement covers Claude Code subscription limits, not API usage.
  • Plans the announcement does not name: the promotion excluded Free plans and consumption-based Enterprise seats; the permanent announcement does not restate those exclusions, so nothing is claimed about them either way here.
  • Pooling: For Pro and Max, Claude apps and Claude Code draw from the same weekly bucket. Team plans give individual member limits, not an org-wide pool.

Claude Pro vs Max limits: what we know

Agencies choosing between Claude Pro and Claude Max often ask how the weekly limits compare. What is verified: both plans are covered by the September 14 change, and each shares a single weekly pool between the Claude apps and Claude Code. What is not published: fixed weekly limit numbers for either plan. Anthropic has never published the absolute values, so treat any specific "requests per week" figure you see elsewhere as an estimate, not an official number. The verified comparison is the ratio — the promotional 150% of baseline, replaced by 125% from September 14, 2026 for both tiers.

5 actions to take now the change has landed

  1. Re-run your budget on the standard cap. Model capacity at 125/150 (0.833×) of the promotional limit. If a workload used 100% of the promotional cap, it is 120% of the standard cap today — decide whether you absorb it, raise retainers, or add headroom. The worked model — seats, credits and the over-cap case — is on Claude Code usage limit cost impact at aiagencycalculator.com.
  2. Rebalance against the new ceiling. The boost ran through September 13, 2026 and is gone. Any spike work still planned against the promotional ceiling needs a new slot — stagger big refactors, batch migrations and client build-outs, or schedule them as usage-credit work.
  3. Review your plan mix and seat count. If you are near the cap weekly, the post-Sept-14 math may justify upgrading seats or adding capacity. Check Settings > Usage to see your real weekly consumption before you decide — do not guess.
  4. Add efficiency tooling. Every message you save is capacity you keep. CLAUDE.md memory files, compact prompts, well-scoped subagents, and caching cut messages-per-task and stretch the weekly bucket further.
  5. Set a review date. The change landed September 14, 2026 — compare usage from before and after it, then adjust client pricing or internal estimates on the new cost-per-task.

None of this requires panic. The change is a capacity re-baseline, not a service interruption — but it landed at budget season for many agencies, which is why the numbers are worth re-running now rather than at the next renewal.
