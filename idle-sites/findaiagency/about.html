About Find AI Agency

Last updated: September 11, 2026

What this site publishes

Find AI Agency is an editorial site about buying AI services. Its pages explain how AI agency work is scoped, priced, contracted and managed — what a project of a given size tends to cost, which contract clauses carry the risk, what to ask a provider before signing, and how to judge whether an automation project is worth doing at all.

The material is written for the buyer's side of the table: a small-business owner, an operations lead, or an in-house team deciding whether to bring in outside help. Each page is a standalone explainer on one question, and where a topic has a companion tool the page links out to the relevant free pricing or cost calculator.

What this site is not

Find AI Agency is not a directory, a marketplace, a review site or a lead-generation service. It publishes no agency profiles, no listings, no rankings, no ratings and no endorsements, and it does not vet, verify, accredit or recommend any agency. Where a company, product or vendor is named inside a guide, it is named as an example in the discussion — that is not a recommendation of it.

There are no city pages and no location-based pages on this site. Coverage here is organised by topic, not by market, and no page on this site makes a claim about coverage in any particular place.

How the pages are written

Each guide is built to answer a question a buyer actually has at the point of deciding: what the work costs, what the agreement should say, what evidence to ask for, and where the risk sits. Figures and claims about outside products are attributed on the page to the source they came from, and each page carries the date it was published so a reader can judge how current it is.

AI products, prices and vendor terms change quickly, so a page can go out of date between updates. Where a page cites a third-party study, vendor announcement or report, the citation is on that page and the source — not this site — is responsible for it.
