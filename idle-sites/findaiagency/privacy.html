Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

What this policy covers

This policy describes what happens to information when you visit findaiagency.com. It covers this site and the pages published on it, and nothing else.

What is collected when you load a page

Serving the page. This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Delivering a page requires the host to receive your request, which includes your IP address and your browser's user-agent string. Cloudflare processes that request data in order to serve the page and to protect the site from abuse and attack traffic.

Analytics. The site loads one third-party analytics script, Umami, served from cloud.umami.is. It receives the page request and reports aggregate, IP-level and user-agent-level usage data back to us — which pages were viewed, roughly when, and the broad source the visit arrived from. We use it only to see which guides are read and which are not. The data is aggregate: it is used to understand traffic to pages, not to identify individual visitors.

What this site does not do

No cookies. Loading a page on this site does not set a cookie. We do not use cookie-based tracking, and there is no cookie banner because there are no cookies to consent to.

No advertising or third-party trackers. There are no advertising scripts on this site, and no tracking pixels from advertising or social networks. The analytics script described above is the only external script the site loads.

No accounts and no forms. This site has no login, no account system, no newsletter signup, no comment section and no form of any kind. There is nowhere on this site to type a name, an email address or a phone number, so no such information is collected. The site also publishes no contact address.

Third-party services

Two outside providers are involved when a page is served: Cloudflare hosts and delivers the site, and Umami provides the analytics described above. Each of those providers handles the request data it processes under its own privacy policy and terms.

Changes to this policy

If the tools this site uses change — for example, if a different analytics provider is adopted or another script is added — this page will be updated to describe the new practice, and the date at the top of the page will change with it. The version published here is the current one; this policy was last updated on September 11, 2026.

Using the content

The rules that apply to the guides published on this site are set out in the Terms of Use, and what this site does and does not publish is described on the About page.
