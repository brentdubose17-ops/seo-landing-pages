Industry Experience Do they have case studies in your niche? Fashion requires different AI than electronics. Ask for examples of similar products, customer bases, or sales cycles. ### 2. Integration Capabilities Your AI agency must work seamlessly with Shopify, Magento, WooCommerce, or BigCommerce. Avoid agencies that need you to switch platforms or build custom APIs from scratch. ### 3. Data Privacy & Compliance AI relies on customer data. Ensure the agency is GDPR, CCPA, and PCI-DSS compliant. They should explain how data is stored, anonymized, and protected. ### 4. Customization vs. Off-the-Shelf Some agencies use pre-built AI tools (less expensive, faster to deploy). Others build custom models (better fit, but pricier and slower). Decide which trade-off fits your goals. ### 5. Post-Launch Support AI isn't "set and forget." Agencies should offer ongoing monitoring, retraining, and optimization. Ask about their support response times and whether they provide performance dashboards. ## Pricing: What to Expect AI agency pricing varies widely based on scope, complexity, and technology.
