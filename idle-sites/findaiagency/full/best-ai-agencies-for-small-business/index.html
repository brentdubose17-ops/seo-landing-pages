What to Look for in an AI Agency for Small Business

Before diving into agency profiles, here are the non-negotiable criteria for evaluating an AI partner when you're a small business:

  • Scalable pricing models: Look for project-based or monthly retainer options under $10,000 per engagement. Avoid agencies that only offer six-figure enterprise contracts.
  • Industry-specific experience: Agencies that have worked with businesses in your niche — retail, healthcare, professional services — can deliver faster results with fewer costly iterations.
  • No-code or low-code capability: Most small businesses lack in-house developers. Agencies using platforms like Make.com, Zapier, Bubble, or GoHighLevel can reduce development time by 40% and keep you independent post-launch.
  • Clear ROI tracking: The agency should provide measurable outcomes tied to cost savings, time recovered, or revenue growth — not vague promises.
  • Post-launch support: Ensure at least 30 days of included support after go-live, with affordable ongoing maintenance options.
