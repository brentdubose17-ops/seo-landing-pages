Anthropic in Talks to Buy Decart for ~$6B: What It Means for AI Agencies

Published August 15, 2026By ABD Legacy LLC
AI industry

What happened. Bloomberg reported on Aug 13, 2026, citing people familiar with the matter, that Anthropic is in early-stage talks to acquire Decart AI for about $6 billion — a deal that would be Anthropic's largest known acquisition, landing ahead of its hotly anticipated IPO. Reuters confirmed the talks within hours. The talks are early, nothing has been signed, and the deal could still fall through — both wires say so explicitly.

Decart is an Israeli-founded (2023), San Francisco-based AI startup with roughly 100 employees. Its headline product is DOS, the Decart Optimization Stack — a hardware-agnostic inference and training platform that Decart claims delivers 1,600 tokens/sec agentic inference (~8x an industry average it puts at ~200) and >80% Model FLOPS Utilization on Amazon Trainium. Those are company/partner claims, not independently audited figures.

Why the deal matters

Bloomberg's framing: Decart's chip-efficiency software “could help Anthropic's existing infrastructure absorb more demand” — the same physical chips serving more Claude queries per dollar. Per Reuters, if the deal closes, Decart's team would join Anthropic's inference and performance organization — the division responsible for how efficiently Claude runs at scale. Vendor M&A like this shifts pricing, roadmaps, and tool risk for the models agencies build on.

Bottom line for agencies

  • Don't reprice on an unclosed deal. No API pricing, rate-limit, or roadmap change is tied to this news. Keep retainers where they are; watch Anthropic's pricing announcements instead.
  • Claude video is a watch item, not a promise. No product decision about Decart's world models (Lucy/Oasis) shipping inside Claude has been reported.
  • Watch DOS licensing. Decart's primary revenue is licensing DOS to cloud providers and AI labs; whether it stays third-party after an acquisition is an open question.
  • Keep the multi-vendor hedge. Nvidia's own advanced talks with Decart reportedly collapsed after higher offers emerged — talks like this don't always close.

What to watch

  1. Whether the deal closes — and at what price. ~$6B is roughly a 50% premium over Decart's ~$4B valuation from May 2026.
  2. Anthropic pricing announcements. If DOS genuinely improves throughput, the plausible effect is fewer capacity crunches and more room to hold — or improve — pricing.
  3. The IPO narrative. Anthropic's reported target of gross margins improving from roughly 40% to 77% by 2028 makes inference efficiency a board-level story, not a footnote.
