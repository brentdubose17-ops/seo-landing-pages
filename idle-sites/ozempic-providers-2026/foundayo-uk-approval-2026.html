Foundayo UK Approval: What Eli Lilly's Oral GLP-1 Pill Means for Patients

Published August 11, 2026By ABD Legacy LLC

The News: UK First in Europe to Approve Foundayo

On August 10, 2026, the UK's Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA) authorised Foundayo (orforglipron), Eli Lilly's once-daily oral GLP-1 receptor agonist — making the UK the first country in Europe to approve the tablet for both weight management and type 2 diabetes. It is the second oral GLP-1 pill cleared by the MHRA, after Novo Nordisk's Wegovy pill (oral semaglutide), which was approved for weight loss only in June 2026.

In the UK, Foundayo is authorised for weight loss and weight maintenance in adults with a BMI of 30 or above — or a BMI between 27 and 30 with at least one weight-related comorbidity — alongside a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity. It is also authorised to improve glycaemic control in adults with insufficiently controlled type 2 diabetes. Treatment starts at 0.8 mg and escalates through 2.5 mg, 5.5 mg, 9 mg, 14.5 mg, and 17.2 mg, with a minimum of one month at each dose level. The MHRA announcement and Reuters' coverage confirm the details.

Why the Oral Option Matters

A pill version of a GLP-1 removes the injection barrier that keeps a large share of eligible patients out of the category. The UK approval is the clearest regulatory validation yet that oral GLP-1s are a real growth engine — and it confirms that both major manufacturers (Novo and Lilly) now have approved tablets in the class. For patients who have avoided injectables, an oral GLP-1 pill opens a path they may not have considered. Foundayo vs. Injectable Semaglutide: What Actually Changes

The useful frame for patients is a three-way comparison: oral orforglipron, oral semaglutide, and injectable semaglutide. Dosing frequency and food rules are the differentiators. Reuters notes that injectables remain the market leaders in the GLP-1 category, "aided by their established efficacy and once-weekly dosing schedules" — so injectable semaglutide stays the efficacy anchor even as the oral options multiply.

UK Availability: Private Prescription First, NICE Review Next

Foundayo will launch in the UK via private prescription later this month (August 2026) — it is not available on the NHS yet. A Lilly spokesperson told Reuters the pill would carry a lower UK list price privately than Mounjaro. Note: wire reports conflict on Mounjaro's current monthly list price (one Reuters copy cites £399, another £330), so treat the comparator as "below Mounjaro's private list price" rather than a single figure. NICE is evaluating Foundayo, with provisional guidance on its use for overweight and obesity expected in mid-November 2026. Foundayo is also under review in the EU.

For US readers, the availability story is different: the FDA approved Foundayo on April 1, 2026, and it has been commercially available in the US since then. The UK decision does not change US availability, price, or coverage — but it does add to the oral GLP-1 shift. When supply or coverage makes an injectable hard to fill, an approved oral option is increasingly part of the alternatives conversation.

FAQ

Q: Is Foundayo available in the US?

A: Yes. The FDA approved Eli Lilly's Foundayo (orforglipron) on April 1, 2026 for adults with obesity or overweight with weight-related medical problems, and it has been commercially available in the US since then. The UK approval does not change US availability.

Q: How is Foundayo different from the Wegovy pill?

A: Both are once-daily oral GLP-1 pills, but Foundayo can be taken at any time of day with no food or water restrictions, while the Wegovy pill must be taken on an empty stomach with a small amount of water, followed by a 30-minute wait before eating or taking other medications.

Q: Is Foundayo as effective as injectable semaglutide?

A: Reuters notes injectables remain the market leaders in the GLP-1 category, aided by their established efficacy and once-weekly dosing schedules. Injectable semaglutide stays the efficacy anchor; the oral pill's chief advantage is convenience.

Q: Can UK patients get Foundayo on the NHS?

A: Not immediately. Foundayo will be available via private prescription in the UK from late August 2026. NICE is evaluating the drug, with provisional guidance on its use for overweight and obesity expected in mid-November 2026.
