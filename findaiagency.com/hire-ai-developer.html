What to look for when you hire AI developer experts

Before you commit, consider these factors to ensure a successful collaboration:

  • Domain experience: Have they built similar solutions (e.g., customer support bots, document processing, predictive models)?
  • Tech stack: Do they use frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, LangChain, or OpenAI APIs? Does it align with your project?
  • Communication & process: How do they handle requirements, milestones, and revisions? Clear workflows prevent costly misunderstandings.
  • Scalability: Can the agency scale up if your project grows? Do they offer ongoing support and maintenance?

💡 Pro tip: Start with a small pilot project to evaluate collaboration before committing to a larger engagement. Most agencies in our directory offer discovery calls and proof-of-concept work.
