Kansas Auto Glass Regulations & Safety Standards

Kansas requires all auto glass replacement to meet Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) 212 for windshield retention and FMVSS 205 for glazing materials. All replacement glass must be ANSI Z26.1 certified. Wichita auto glass shops must be registered with the Kansas Department of Revenue and carry $300,000 in general liability insurance.

For vehicles with ADAS features (lane departure warning, automatic emergency braking, adaptive cruise control) — increasingly common on 2018+ vehicles — Kansas law requires recalibration after windshield replacement. Recalibration costs $200–$500 and is not always covered by insurance. Skipping ADAS recalibration can cause safety system errors and void your vehicle warranty. Always verify the shop has ADAS calibration equipment before booking a modern vehicle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Kansas require zero-deductible windshield replacement?

No, Kansas law does not mandate zero-deductible glass coverage. However, many insurers offer it as an add-on or include it in comprehensive policies. Check your policy or call your agent.

How long does windshield replacement take in Wichita?

Installation takes 30–60 minutes. The adhesive needs 60–90 minutes to cure before you can drive. Mobile technicians typically complete the full job at your location within 2 hours.

Can I drive immediately after windshield replacement?

Wait at least 60 minutes before driving to allow the urethane adhesive to cure. Avoid potholes, rough roads, and car washes for 24 hours. Summer heat speeds curing; cold weather extends it.

How do I know if my car needs ADAS recalibration?

Any vehicle with a forward-facing camera behind the windshield (2018+ models with lane keep, adaptive cruise, or auto braking) needs recalibration after replacement. Ask your glass shop to verify.

Does insurance cover hail-damaged auto glass in Kansas?

Yes — comprehensive coverage pays for hail damage minus your deductible. Some insurers offer $0 deductible glass add-ons. Chips repaired within 30 days are often covered at no cost.
