What Counts as a Plumbing Emergency?

Not every dripping faucet is an emergency. Call a 24/7 plumber for: burst or leaking pipes (can cause $5,000+ in water damage within hours), sewer backups (raw sewage is a health hazard), gas line leaks (smell of rotten eggs — evacuate and call 911 first), water heater failures (especially gas units with suspected leaks), and complete toilet blockage when you have only one bathroom.

How Nashville's Hard Water Affects Your Plumbing

Middle Tennessee has moderately hard to hard water (7–10 grains per gallon). This causes mineral buildup inside pipes, reducing water flow by up to 25% after 5 years and dramatically shortening water heater lifespan. If you're calling for a second water heater repair in 3 years, hard water scale is likely the culprit. A whole-home water softener ($1,200–$2,800 installed) pays for itself in appliance longevity.
