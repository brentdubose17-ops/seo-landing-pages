What Does a Small Business Accountant Cost in Sacramento?

Monthly bookkeeping runs $350–$900 depending on transaction volume. Tax preparation for an LLC or S-Corp: $1,000–$3,000 including California FTB and federal returns. California has the highest tax preparation costs in the nation due to state-specific complexity (Form 100 or 100S, FTB schedules, SB 137). Quarterly estimated tax calculation and filing: $200–$400 per quarter. Many Sacramento firms offer flat-fee packages combining bookkeeping + tax prep for $600–$1,500/month.

California Business Taxes You Cannot Ignore

California imposes an 8.84% corporate franchise tax on S-Corps and C-Corps — one of the highest in the country. LLCs pay an annual $800 franchise tax plus a gross receipts-based fee ranging $0–$12,000. The state personal income tax brackets run 1%–13.3%. Sales tax in Sacramento County is 8.75%. Late filings with the FTB carry 5% penalties plus 0.5% monthly interest, and the EDD aggressively pursues misclassified independent contractors with back taxes and penalties averaging $5,000–$50,000 per violation.

When to Hire an Accountant vs. DIY in Sacramento

If your business clears under $50K with no employees and simple finances, QuickBooks or Wave may work. Hire a CPA when revenue exceeds $100K, you have employees, you're filing as an S-Corp or multi-member LLC, or you've received an FTB or IRS notice. California's tax complexity means that DIY tax preparation errors are especially costly — fixing a misclassified filing status with the FTB can cost 2–3x the original tax liability in penalties and interest.
