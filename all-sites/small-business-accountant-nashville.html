Tennessee Business Taxes: What You Actually Pay

Tennessee is famously tax-friendly: no state income tax on wages or salaries (the Hall income tax on dividends was fully repealed in 2021), and no state personal income tax at all. But businesses pay the franchise and excise (F&E) tax — currently 6.5% on net earnings or a $100 minimum, plus a franchise tax based on the greater of net worth or property value.

Sales tax is the other major cost: Tennessee's state rate is 7%, and Davidson County adds 2.25% for a combined 9.25% — among the highest in the nation. Getting nexus and sales tax collection right matters: a Nashville e-commerce business shipping nationwide must register and file in multiple states. An accountant handles this so you don't face surprise liabilities.

LLC vs S-Corp in Tennessee: Which Saves You More?

Most Nashville small businesses start as LLCs (formation: $300 state fee, minimal paperwork). But once profits exceed $40,000–$60,000, an S-corp election usually saves $3,000–$8,000/year in self-employment taxes. An S-corp owner takes a "reasonable salary" (subject to FICA) and the remaining profit as distributions (not subject to self-employment tax).

Tennessee S-corps also face the F&E tax on net earnings — but the 6.5% rate applies only to earnings, and proper salary structuring minimizes the combined burden. A Nashville CPA models both scenarios with your actual numbers; the decision depends on profit level, owner headcount, and long-term plans.

When to Hire an Accountant in Nashville

Hire an accountant when you: 1) launch your business (formation + structure), 2) hire your first employee (payroll compliance), 3) cross $100K in revenue (S-corp analysis), 4) sell products online (multi-state sales tax nexus), or 5) get an IRS or Tennessee Department of Revenue notice. Waiting until April 14 is the most expensive strategy — tax planning must happen year-round.

The IRS audits small businesses at a ~1% rate nationally, but deduction documentation failures are the #1 audit finding. Nashville accountants maintain the paper trail — mileage logs, home office calculations, equipment purchases — so your deductions survive scrutiny. A $300–$600 annual investment routinely saves $3,000+ in legal deductions and penalties avoided.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Tennessee have a state income tax for businesses?

No personal income tax, but businesses pay the franchise and excise tax at 6.5% of net earnings (or $100 minimum) plus franchise tax on net worth. An accountant ensures you pay only what's owed.

How much does it cost to start an LLC in Nashville?

Tennessee charges $300 to file Articles of Organization, plus a $50 minimum franchise tax. Registered agent services run $50–$150/year. Accountant setup fees for formation + EIN + books run $300–$800.

Should I elect S-corp status for my Nashville business?

If your LLC profits exceed $40,000–$60,000, S-corp election typically saves $3,000–$8,000/year in self-employment taxes. Your accountant models the math with your real numbers before you file Form 2553.

What sales tax rate do Nashville businesses charge?

9.25% — Tennessee's 7% state rate plus Davidson County's 2.25% local rate. Online sellers need nexus review: shipping into other states may create filing obligations there.

Can an accountant help me if I'm behind on taxes?

Yes — accountants handle back filings, penalty abatement, and IRS installment agreements. Acting before a levy or lien is filed dramatically improves your options; don't wait for the notice deadline.
