Licensed, insured, and available 24/7 for AC and furnace repairs across Columbus, Franklin County, and the surrounding central Ohio metro.

How Much Does HVAC Repair Cost in Columbus?

Columbus homeowners pay $130–$850 for common HVAC repairs. Diagnostic fees run $79–$110 and are usually waived if you proceed with the repair. A capacitor replacement costs $150–$320, blower motor replacement runs $400–$850, and a new gas furnace installation costs $3,500–$6,500. Heat pump systems, increasingly popular in central Ohio, run $5,000–$9,500 installed.

Ohio winters drive peak furnace demand between December and February, when Columbus averages 25+ nights below 20°F. Summer AC calls spike in July and August when temperatures hit 90°F+ with humidity above 60%. Expect 10–15% higher pricing during peak seasonal months due to demand.

What Are Ohio's HVAC Licensing Requirements?

Ohio requires HVAC contractors to hold a license from the Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board (OCILB). Licenses require at least 2 years of verified experience or equivalent technical education, plus a passing score on the state trade exam. Contractors must carry general liability insurance and renew their license annually.

The City of Columbus requires a mechanical permit for all HVAC installations over $1,000, with inspection fees of $60–$150. Franklin County performs the final inspection before the system can be operated. Work without a permit risks fines of $500–$2,000. Verify any contractor's OCILB license number through the state's online license lookup before scheduling work.

When Should You Repair vs. Replace Your Columbus HVAC System?

If your system is under 10 years old and the repair costs less than 50% of a new unit, repairing is the financially smarter move. For systems 15 years or older — especially the aging R-22 systems still common in Columbus's older homes — replacement is typically recommended. R-22 refrigerant has not been produced since 2020, so recharging an old R-22 system now costs $600–$1,200 per service visit.

Since January 1, 2025, the EPA's refrigerant transition requires all new systems to use A2L refrigerants like R-454B or R-32 instead of R-410A. Federal tax credits of up to 30% on qualifying high-efficiency systems (max $2,000) and Ohio's utility rebates bring the payback period on a new system to 3–5 years.

How Does Columbus's Climate Affect Your HVAC System?

Columbus sits in a humid continental climate with one of the widest seasonal swings in the Midwest. Summers reach 90°F+ on about 20 days per year with humidity frequently above 60%, while winter lows drop below 20°F on 25+ nights. That 70°F+ swing means your system runs near capacity in both extremes.

Freeze-thaw cycles are hard on outdoor condensers and heat pumps, and Columbus's older housing stock — including the brick homes of German Village and Clintonville — often has aging ductwork that leaks 20–30% of conditioned air. A duct sealing service, typically $400–$900, is one of the highest-ROI upgrades for central Ohio homes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I get HVAC service in Columbus?

Most licensed Columbus HVAC companies offer same-day emergency service. Response times average 2–4 hours across the metro, including Dublin, Westerville, and Grove City.

Does Ohio require HVAC contractors to be licensed?

Yes. All HVAC contractors must hold an Ohio HVAC license from the OCILB. Columbus also requires a mechanical permit for installations over $1,000. Verify through the OCILB online portal.

What's the average lifespan of an HVAC system in Columbus?

A well-maintained central AC lasts 10–14 years in Ohio's climate. Gas furnaces typically last 18–22 years. Bi-annual maintenance (spring + fall) extends lifespan by 3–5 years.

Is emergency HVAC service more expensive in Columbus?

Emergency after-hours service adds $75–$150 on top of standard rates. Weekend and holiday calls may cost an additional $100–$200 during winter cold snaps.

Do I need a permit for HVAC replacement in Columbus?

Yes. Columbus city requires a mechanical permit for all HVAC system replacements. Permit fees are $60–$150 and a final inspection is mandatory before the system can be operated.
