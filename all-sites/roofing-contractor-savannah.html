Roofing Challenges in Savannah's Coastal Climate

Savannah's salt-laden air, 90°F+ summers and 75%+ humidity accelerate shingle degradation. Asphalt shingles that last 15–20 years in drier climates fail in 10–12 years in Savannah. Algae and mold grow on shingles in the high humidity, which is cosmetic but can trap moisture and accelerate deterioration. Metal roofs can develop white corrosion (zinc corrosion) if the paint finish wears off, exposing bare metal to salt air.

Thunderstorms in Savannah bring high winds (30–50 mph), hail and heavy rain. Wind damage to roofs shows up as lifted shingles, missing tabs and exposed underlayment. Hail damage creates small dents and punctures in the shingles that may not be visible from the ground but allow water infiltration over time. After a storm, a professional roof inspection can identify damage that qualifies for an insurance claim.

Hurricane season (June–November) brings tropical storms with sustained winds 39+ mph. A roof that is 8+ years old and showing signs of wear is at higher risk of losing shingles or sections during a hurricane. A new roof or roof reinforcement (hurricane straps, impact-resistant shingles) is a good investment in Savannah.

Georgia Roofing Contractor Licensing and Permit Requirements

Georgia does not require state-level licensing for roofers, but any roofer working on a residential project must carry a current Georgia Construction Contractor License (CCL) for the scope of work (residential, commercial or industrial). The Savannah building department and Chatham County also require a building permit for roof replacement or major repairs. The permit ensures that the new roof meets current building code, including wind resistance and ventilation requirements.

A roof replacement permit in Savannah costs $100–$300 and takes 5–10 business days to approve. The contractor typically handles the permit and includes the cost in the overall estimate. After the roof is installed, a city inspector must verify that the work meets code before the permit is closed out.

Georgia has no state incentive for installing a metal roof or impact-resistant shingles, but the federal government and some insurance companies offer discounts. Some insurers reduce premiums for homes with impact-resistant shingles (Class 4 or better) because they withstand hail and wind better than standard shingles.

Roof Repair vs. Replacement: When to Choose Each Option

If your roof is under 10 years old and has isolated damage (a few missing shingles, a small leak), repair is the right choice. A patch or shingle replacement costs $200–$800 depending on the location and number of shingles needed. If the damage is widespread or the roof is over 12 years old, replacement is usually more economical than repeated repairs.

Asphalt shingle replacement costs $4,500–$10,000 depending on roof size, pitch and material choice. A standard 30-year architectural shingle costs $4,500–$7,500 installed. Metal roofing (standing seam or metal shingles) costs $8,000–$14,000 installed but lasts 40–50 years. A metal roof qualifies for some insurance discounts in Georgia, and the longer lifespan makes it economical over the long run.

After a storm, insurance may cover the cost of replacement if the damage is deemed storm-related (wind, hail). The insurance adjuster will inspect the roof, estimate the cost and issue a claim. The homeowner and contractor then work together to complete the replacement and submit proof of completion to the insurance company.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an asphalt shingle roof last in Savannah?

10–12 years due to high humidity and salt air. In drier climates, they last 15–20 years. Regular maintenance (cleaning, inspections) can extend the lifespan.

What is an impact-resistant shingle?

Class 4 impact-resistant shingles can withstand larger hail and heavy objects without cracking or puncturing. They cost 10–20% more than standard shingles but may reduce insurance premiums.

Do I need a permit for roof replacement?

Yes. Savannah and Chatham County require a permit for roof replacement or major repairs. The contractor handles the permit process; cost is $100–$300 and typically included in the estimate.

How do I file an insurance claim for roof damage?

Contact your insurance company and request an adjuster inspection. We provide photos, damage assessment and estimates to support your claim. Most policies cover wind and hail damage.

How much does a new roof cost in Savannah?

Asphalt shingles: $4,500–$10,000. Metal roofing: $8,000–$14,000. Costs depend on roof size, pitch and material choice.
