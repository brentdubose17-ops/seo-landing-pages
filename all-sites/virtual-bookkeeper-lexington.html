Virtual Bookkeeping: What It Is and Why It's Affordable

Virtual bookkeeping is remote accounting services delivered online. Instead of hiring an in-house bookkeeper or accountant, you pay a virtual bookkeeper a monthly fee (typically $300–$800) to handle your accounting. The virtual bookkeeper connects to your bank and credit card accounts through secure software links, categorizes transactions and produces monthly financial statements. You retain access to all data and reports, and you can switch providers with no long-term contract.

The cost savings are significant. A part-time in-house bookkeeper costs $20,000–$35,000 per year (salary + taxes), plus health insurance and benefits. A virtual bookkeeper costs $3,600–$9,600 per year. You also gain flexibility: if your business is slow in winter, you can scale back bookkeeping hours. If you grow, you can increase to tax planning and payroll management without hiring additional staff.

Virtual bookkeeping is secure. QuickBooks Online, Xero and other platforms use bank-level encryption. You control access and can monitor which employees see what data. Some business owners worry that a remote bookkeeper might lose data or cause problems, but cloud-based software has automatic backups and compliance audits. Data loss is rare.

Kentucky Tax Considerations for Small Business Bookkeeping

Kentucky's individual income tax rate is flat at 3.5% (as of 2026; it was lowered from 4.0% on January 1, 2025). For small business owners, this applies to pass-through income (S-corp, LLC, sole proprietor). Kentucky corporations pay 5.85% on net income. The tax environment is moderate compared to other states, but compliance is still required: quarterly estimated tax payments, annual state income tax returns, and proper payroll withholding.

Lexington imposes a local payroll tax for businesses with 5+ employees: the Lexington-Fayette Urban County payroll tax is 1.4% of wages paid. If you are considering hiring your first employees, budget for this tax. Sole proprietors and small business owners (fewer than 5 employees) may not be subject, depending on structure.

Kentucky has no sales tax on services, but tangible goods sales are taxed at 6% (plus local option surtax). If you sell products, you must register for a sales tax permit and file monthly sales tax returns. Virtual bookkeeping services (professional services) are not taxed in Kentucky, so no sales tax collection is required on your bookkeeping income.

Cloud Accounting Platforms: QuickBooks, Xero and Wave Integration

QuickBooks Online is the most popular accounting software for small businesses. It connects to your bank and credit card accounts, lets you invoice customers and track expenses, and produces financial reports. QuickBooks Plus (for small businesses) costs $99–$165 per month. We set up your chart of accounts, connect your bank accounts, and manage your books from there. You can log in anytime to see your cash position and profit/loss.

Xero is a simpler alternative popular with service businesses and freelancers. Xero costs $9–$60 per month depending on the plan, and it includes invoicing, expense tracking and bank reconciliation. Some businesses use both: QuickBooks for complex reporting and payroll, Xero for simple invoice and expense management.

Wave is the most affordable option: it is free for basic invoicing and expense tracking. Wave is ideal for freelancers or very small service businesses with minimal complexity. Wave does not have payroll processing, so for businesses with employees, QuickBooks or Xero is needed.
