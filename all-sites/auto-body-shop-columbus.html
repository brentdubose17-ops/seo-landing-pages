Collision Repair Costs in Columbus

Minor dent and scratch repair runs $250–$800. Major collision work (bumper replacement, quarter panel, frame straightening) ranges $1,500–$6,000 depending on parts and labor. Columbus labor rates average $55–$75 per hour for body work and $85–$120 for structural frame repair. Most shops provide free estimates and work directly with your insurance adjuster.

Paint Matching — Why It Matters in Ohio Winters

Ohio's road salt and freeze-thaw cycles accelerate paint failure. A proper paint job uses a spectrophotometer to read your car's original color, accounts for fade, and blends into adjacent panels so the repair is invisible. Shops that skip blending or use generic color codes produce visible mismatches that become obvious in direct sunlight.

Insurance Claims in Ohio — What You Need to Know

Ohio is an at-fault state. If the other driver caused the accident, their liability insurance covers your repairs. If you carry collision coverage, your insurer pays minus your deductible and subrogates against the at-fault driver. A good body shop handles the insurance paperwork and supplements (additional repairs found during disassembly) so you don't have to argue with the adjuster.
