Texas Plumber in El Paso — Licensing and Rules

Texas requires all plumbers to be licensed by the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners. A Master Plumber license requires 4 years of apprenticeship (8,000 hours) plus 2 years as a journeyman. Always verify the license number through the TSBPE website. El Paso also requires a city permit for any plumbing work exceeding $5,000. Be wary of unlicensed "handymen" offering emergency plumbing — Texas law prohibits anyone other than a licensed plumber from performing plumbing work for compensation, and insurance may deny claims from unlicensed work.

El Paso's Hard Water — The Silent Pipe Killer

El Paso has some of the hardest water in the United States — over 250 ppm of dissolved minerals. This causes scale buildup inside pipes, reducing water flow by 10–20% per year and shortening water heater life by 3–5 years. If you notice low water pressure, white deposits on faucets, or your water heater running longer to produce hot water, hard water scale is the likely culprit. A whole-house water softener ($600–$2,500 installed) pays for itself in extended appliance life and reduced plumbing repairs within 3 years in El Paso.
