Utah's Winter Road Hazards

Utah's heavy snowfall means road salt and sand are used extensively from November through March. This accelerates rust on older vehicles and can cause paint damage on repaired panels that weren't properly sealed. The freeze-thaw cycle creates potholes that bend wheels and damage suspension components. Many SLC auto body shops offer winter-preparation inspections that catch rust-prone areas before they spread.

Utah Insurance — At-Fault State Rules

Utah is a no-fault insurance state with a $3,000 PIP threshold. This means your own insurance pays for your medical bills up to $3,000 regardless of fault. For vehicle damage, the at-fault driver's property damage liability covers your repairs. If you carry collision coverage, your insurer pays minus your deductible and pursues subrogation against the at-fault party. A good body shop handles the insurance paperwork and supplemental approvals so you don't have to negotiate with the adjuster.
