New York State Taxes for Small Business: Corporate, Payroll and Sales Tax

New York State's corporate tax rate is 6.85% for C-corporations and a flat 6.85% for pass-through entities (S-corps, LLCs taxed as partnerships). Combined with federal tax (21% for C-corps, pass-through rates vary), New York State small businesses face some of the highest state tax burdens in the US. A strategic business structure (C-corp vs S-corp vs. LLC) can save thousands per year. We help you choose the best structure for your specific business.

Albany County's sales tax is 8.875%, which is collected on most retail sales. If you sell taxable goods or services, you must register for a New York State sales tax permit and file monthly sales tax returns. Failure to collect and remit sales tax is a serious offense that can result in penalties equal to 10% of uncollected tax plus interest.

Payroll taxes include federal income tax withholding, Social Security (6.2% employee + 6.2% employer), Medicare (1.45% employee + 1.45% employer) plus New York State income tax and unemployment insurance. Employers must file quarterly payroll tax returns (Form 941 federally, plus NY state filings) and annual W-2s. We handle payroll processing and tax filing to ensure compliance.

Business Formation and Startup Tax Planning

When you form a business in New York, you must choose a legal structure: sole proprietorship, LLC, S-corporation, or C-corporation. Each has different tax implications. A sole proprietor pays self-employment tax (15.3% of net income) plus income tax. An LLC can be taxed as a partnership (pass-through) or as a corporation. An S-corp pays only the owner's salary to self-employment tax; profits distributed as dividends avoid self-employment tax but require separate payroll processing.

An S-corp election can save a business owner $5,000–$20,000 per year in self-employment taxes, depending on business income. However, S-corp status requires quarterly payroll tax filings and annual tax returns, which adds accounting costs. We calculate whether S-corp status makes sense for your business based on income projections.

New York requires business formation filings: Articles of Organization for LLCs, Articles of Incorporation for corporations, or a DBA (Doing Business As) registration for sole proprietors. We handle these filings and ensure you comply with state regulations. Business licenses, industry-specific permits and federal tax ID (EIN) applications are also part of startup setup.

Bookkeeping, Quarterly Estimated Taxes and Year-End Tax Planning

Accurate bookkeeping is the foundation of tax compliance and financial management. We record all revenue and expenses, reconcile bank and credit card accounts, and categorize transactions for tax and management reporting. Monthly financial statements show you your profit margin, cash flow and which products or services are most profitable.

Quarterly estimated tax payments (Form 1040-ES for self-employed, Form 1120-ES for corporations) are due April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15. If you do not pay estimated taxes quarterly, the IRS assesses penalties and interest. We calculate your estimated tax liability and help you make timely payments to avoid penalties.

Year-end tax planning identifies opportunities to reduce your tax bill before December 31: retirement plan contributions (SEP-IRA, Solo 401k), business expense timing, equipment depreciation and charitable donations. We prepare your tax return and work backward to identify additional deductions or strategies. A tax return should never be a surprise—you should know your approximate tax liability months in advance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best business structure for tax purposes in New York?

It depends on your income and business type. An S-corp can save $5,000–$20,000/year in self-employment taxes, but the accounting cost is higher. We model different structures for your situation.

Do I need to make quarterly estimated tax payments?

Yes, if you expect to owe $1,000 or more in taxes (self-employed or pass-through business). Payments are due April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15.

How much is New York State's corporate tax rate?

6.85% for both C-corporations and pass-through entities (S-corps, LLCs taxed as partnerships). Plus federal tax (21% for C-corps).

What is Albany County's sales tax rate?

8.875%. If you sell taxable goods or services, you must collect and remit sales tax monthly.

Can I save money with an S-corp election?

Yes, in most cases. An S-corp can save $5,000–$20,000/year in self-employment taxes. The additional accounting cost is typically $2,000–$3,000/year, so there is a net savings.
