Does Insurance Cover Auto Glass in California?

California does not have a zero-deductible windshield law (that's Florida). Coverage depends on your policy: comprehensive coverage pays for glass damage minus your deductible, typically $100–$500. Some insurers offer a 'full glass' endorsement with a $0 deductible for a few dollars a month — worth adding in LA, where chips are constant.

Your insurer cannot force you to use their preferred shop. You can choose any registered glass company, and the shop will usually handle the claim paperwork and bill the insurer directly. Just confirm your deductible in advance so there's no surprise.

ADAS Calibration After Windshield Replacement

Newer cars use the windshield-mounted camera for lane-keeping, automatic emergency braking, and adaptive cruise control. When the windshield is replaced, that camera must be recalibrated — either static (in-shop, targets) or dynamic (test-drive) calibration. Skipping it can leave your safety systems pointing at the wrong angle.

Calibration adds $200–$400 to the job. If your insurer is paying, make sure the shop's estimate includes it; if you're paying out of pocket, it's still worth it — a misaligned camera can brake or steer unexpectedly at freeway speed. Ask the shop which method they use and whether they guarantee the calibration on reinstall.

Chip vs. Full Replacement — When to Repair

Most LA shops repair chips if they're smaller than a quarter, not in the driver's direct line of sight, and not at the edge of the glass. A $50–$130 repair takes 30 minutes, keeps your factory seal, and stops the crack from spreading.

Replace when the crack is longer than 6 inches, crosses the driver's vision, or reaches the edge — repairs on edge cracks almost always fail. Also replace if the glass is delaminating or the previous repair was done badly. Heat speeds crack growth, so don't 'wait and see' through a LA summer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can I get my windshield replaced in LA?

Most shops offer same-day or next-day mobile service. A standard replacement takes 45–90 minutes once the tech arrives.

Can I drive immediately after windshield replacement?

Wait at least 1 hour, and ideally 24, before driving fast or through a car wash so the adhesive cures. Your tech will give specific timing.

Does a cracked windshield fail a California inspection?

Yes — any crack in the driver's line of sight is a safety violation, and significant cracks can fail the vehicle inspection. Don't put it off.

What if my car has HUD or rain sensors?

The shop needs the correct glass with the sensor bracket and HUD-compatible laminate. Confirm they stock OEM-spec glass for your trim level.

Is mobile windshield replacement safe?

Yes — reputable shops use the same OEM-spec adhesive and curing process on-site. The main risk is rushing; choose a shop that guarantees the bond.
