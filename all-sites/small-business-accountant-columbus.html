What Taxes Do Small Businesses Pay in Ohio?

Ohio moved to a flat 2.75% individual income tax in 2026 — one of the simplest structures in the country. The Commercial Activity Tax (CAT) applies a flat 0.26% on taxable gross receipts, but the threshold rises to $6 million for 2026, so most Columbus small businesses owe nothing — though registration can still be required.

The part new owners miss is local: Columbus levies a municipal income tax (typically 2%–2.5%) on income earned or work performed in the city, and the combined sales tax in Franklin County is 8.00% (5.75% state + 2.25% local). A Columbus accountant wires all of these together so you never double-pay or miss a credit.

Should You Hire an Accountant or Use Software?

Software handles data entry; an accountant handles judgment. If your business has employees, inventory, or revenue above roughly $100,000, a professional pays for itself in tax savings and avoided penalties. Most Columbus businesses use both — software (QuickBooks, Xero) for day-to-day flow, an accountant for tax strategy, payroll compliance, and year-end.

The real cost of DIY shows up at tax time: misclassified workers trigger back payroll taxes, uncollected sales tax becomes a personal liability, and missed city income tax credits cost real money. An accountant turns your bookkeeping into a compliance system, not just a record of what happened.

When Are Business Taxes Due in Columbus?

S-corporations and partnerships file by March 15, individuals and sole proprietors by April 15. Ohio follows federal deadlines for most filings, and extensions are available — but estimated taxes are still due quarterly (April 15, June 15, September 15, January 15) regardless of extension.

A good accountant builds your quarterly calendar at setup so you never face a surprise penalty. For a new business, that calendar starts with entity selection and Ohio registration — steps that cost a few hundred dollars in professional fees upfront and save thousands in late-filing penalties and missed elections later.
