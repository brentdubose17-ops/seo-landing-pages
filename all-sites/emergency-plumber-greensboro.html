Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can a plumber respond to an emergency in Greensboro?

Our emergency dispatch team arrives within 45 to 60 minutes across Greensboro, High Point, and Jamestown.

What should I do first when a pipe bursts in my Greensboro home?

Immediately shut off your home's main water valve—usually located near the water meter or crawlspace entry—and call our emergency plumbers.

Do I need a permit for a water heater replacement in Greensboro, NC?

Yes, City of Greensboro building codes require a plumbing permit for water heater installations to guarantee safety and compliance.

How do I know if my sewer line has tree root intrusion?

Common signs include multiple clogged drains, gurgling toilets, foul sewage odors in the yard, or wet spots near tree lines.

Are your plumbers licensed and insured in North Carolina?

Yes, all work is performed by fully licensed NC State Plumbing Contractors holding active general liability and worker's compensation insurance.
