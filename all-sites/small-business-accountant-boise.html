Idaho Tax Regulations & S-Corp Advantage

Idaho imposes a flat individual and corporate income tax rate of 5.8%, along with a 6.0% state sales tax. Businesses operating within Ada County and the City of Boise must also comply with local business personal property tax reporting and sales tax exemption rules.

For LLCs generating over $60,000 in net profit, electing S-Corporation tax status can save owners thousands of dollars annually in self-employment taxes (FICA). Our Boise accountants handle S-Corp payroll setup, Reasonable Compensation calculations, and Form 1120-S filings seamlessly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the corporate tax rate in Idaho for small businesses?

Idaho has a flat 5.8% corporate and individual income tax rate, applied to state net taxable income.

How can electing S-Corp status save my Boise LLC money?

An S-Corp election allows owner-operators to split earnings into reasonable salary and dividend distributions, significantly reducing 15.3% self-employment taxes.

Do you handle Idaho sales tax reporting for online and retail sales?

Yes, we handle monthly and quarterly Idaho sales tax calculation, filing, and state exemption certificate management.

Which accounting software do you support?

How often will I receive financial statements for my company?
