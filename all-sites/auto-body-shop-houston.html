How Much Does Auto Body Repair Cost in Houston?

Houston repair pricing varies by damage and paint type. Bumper repair or replacement runs $500–$1,800, a fender $700–$2,200, a single door dent $900–$2,500, and paintless dent removal (PDR) $150–$600 per panel. Full hail damage repair on a typical sedan runs $3,000–$8,000+, and structural or unibody damage starts around $4,000.

Most reputable Houston shops provide free written estimates within 24 hours and work directly with insurers. Because Houston is a major insurance market, most shops are DRP (direct repair program) approved — meaning the insurer's adjuster trusts their estimates and your claim moves faster with no haggling on your end.

Why Is Houston So Bad for Hail Damage?

Houston sits in one of the most active hail zones in the country. Spring storms (March–May) regularly drop 1–2-inch hailstones, and the metro's sheer vehicle count makes it a top market for hail claims year after year. Hail dents on hoods, roofs, and trunks are the most common repair in the city.

PDR is the preferred fix for hail: technicians massage dents out from behind the panel, preserving the factory paint. It's faster (1–3 days vs 5–7 for repaint) and cheaper ($150–$600 per panel vs $1,000+ for a repaint panel). If the hail cracked the paint, replacement or repaint is required — a good shop tells you honestly which applies.

Do Auto Body Shops in Texas Need a License?

Texas does not issue a statewide license for auto body or collision repair shops — which makes vetting more important, not less. Look for ASE-certified technicians, I-CAR Gold Class certification, a City of Houston business permit, and DRP approval from major insurers like State Farm or GEICO.

Also be alert to flood-damaged inventory: after hurricanes like Beryl, Houston sees a wave of flooded vehicles. Texas brands flood-damaged titles, so if you're buying used, check the title branding and run a history report — and have a body shop inspect for mud residue and corrosion in hard-to-reach spots.

How Long Will My Car Be in the Shop?

Most Houston collision repairs are completed in 3–7 days. PDR hail jobs often finish in 1–3 days. Structural or frame work runs 10–14 days, and parts delays for newer or rare vehicles can add 2–5 days on top. Ask about rental car assistance — most DRP-approved shops include rental coordination through your insurer.

Timelines should be in writing before you authorize work. A reputable shop also photographs the damage with you at drop-off, so there's no dispute about pre-existing condition when you pick the car up.

Can hail damage be fixed without repainting?

Yes — paintless dent repair (PDR) removes small-to-medium hail dents without repainting, keeping your factory finish intact. PDR works best when the paint hasn't cracked. Houston's spring hailstorms generate thousands of PDR claims every year.

How do I know if a Houston used car has flood damage?

Check the Texas title for a flood brand, inspect for mud in crevices, musty odor, or corrosion on connectors, and run a vehicle history report. After major storms like Hurricane Beryl, flood-damaged vehicles enter the Houston market quickly.
