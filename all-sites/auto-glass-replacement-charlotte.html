Auto Glass Replacement in Charlotte, NC

Last updated: July 2026 · Serving Charlotte and surrounding areas

A cracked windshield compromises your vehicle's structural integrity and obstructs your view of the road. In Charlotte, where I-77 construction debris and I-485 gravel trucks are constant hazards, windshield damage is almost routine. The good news: most chip repairs are free through insurance, and mobile auto glass replacement comes to your home or office.

Charlotte Auto Glass Repair Costs

Windshield chip repair: $0–$50 (most insurers waive the deductible for chip repair to prevent costlier full replacement). Windshield replacement: $180–$400 for standard models with ADAS calibration adding $100–$250. Rear or side window replacement: $150–$350 with labor. Mobile service typically adds no extra fee in the Charlotte metro area.

North Carolina Insurance Rules for Auto Glass

North Carolina requires comprehensive coverage insurers to offer zero-deductible glass repair. Full windshield replacement typically falls under comprehensive with your standard deductible ($100–$500). Some insurers (GEICO, Progressive, State Farm) offer $0 deductible glass endorsement for a few dollars per month. Always check your policy before paying out of pocket — most Charlotte drivers overpay by not knowing their glass coverage.

ADAS Calibration — The Hidden Auto Glass Requirement

Modern vehicles with lane departure warnings, automatic emergency braking, and adaptive cruise control require camera recalibration after windshield replacement. Skipping recalibration means those safety systems will malfunction. In Charlotte, ADAS recalibration adds $100–$250 and is covered by insurance when the windshield replacement is a comprehensive claim. Always ask your glass shop: "Will you recalibrate the cameras?"
