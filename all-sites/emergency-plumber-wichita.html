Kansas Plumbing License Requirements for Wichita

Kansas requires all plumbers to hold a state-issued Plumbing License through the Kansas State Board of Technical Professions. Journeyman plumbers need 4 years of apprenticeship under a licensed master plumber, while master plumbers need 2 additional years of licensed work experience plus a passing score on the state board exam.

Wichita city requires a separate city business license for all plumbing contractors operating within city limits. Wichita Water Quality & Its Effect on Plumbing

Wichita's water comes primarily from the Equus Beds aquifer and Cheney Reservoir, with a hardness rating of 12–15 grains per gallon (classified as "very hard"). This mineral content causes accelerated scale buildup in water heaters, reducing efficiency by 15–25% and shortening tank water heater lifespan by 2–4 years compared to national averages in soft-water regions.

Sedgwick County recommends whole-house water softeners for any home with hardness above 10 gpg — roughly 70% of Wichita households qualify. Water heater anode rod replacement every 3 years ($150–$300 installed) can extend tank life by 5+ years in Wichita's hard water conditions. Tankless water heaters are less affected by hard water scale but still need annual descaling ($200–$400).
