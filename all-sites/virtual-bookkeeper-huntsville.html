Frequently Asked Questions

How does virtual bookkeeping work for a Huntsville small business?

You grant secure read-only access to your business bank accounts and QuickBooks. We reconcile accounts, classify expenses, and deliver financial reports remotely every month.

What is the difference between a virtual bookkeeper and a CPA?

A bookkeeper manages daily transaction recording, account reconciliation, and invoicing, while a CPA handles high-level tax planning, audits, and income tax return filing.

Is virtual bookkeeping safe and secure for financial data?

Yes, we utilize bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption, multi-factor authentication, and secure read-only API bank connections.

Can you clean up messy bookkeeping from prior tax years?

Yes, we specialize in catch-up bookkeeping and QuickBooks cleanup to bring past-due accounts up to date for tax filing.

What software do I need for virtual bookkeeping?

We primarily work with QuickBooks Online and Xero. If you don't have an account, we can set one up with wholesale discount pricing.
