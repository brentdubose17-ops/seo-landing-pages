Ohio Sales Tax — The Trap New Business Owners Miss

Ohio requires any business selling tangible goods or certain services to register for a sales tax permit with the Ohio Department of Taxation. The state's CAT (Commercial Activity Tax) applies if your gross receipts exceed $150,000 — even if you had no profit. Virtual bookkeepers catch these requirements before you hit the penalty stage, which is common among new Columbus businesses that start selling online or at local markets.
