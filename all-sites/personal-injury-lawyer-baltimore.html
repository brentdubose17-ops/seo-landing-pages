How Much Is a Baltimore Personal Injury Case Worth?

Settlement values depend on medical expenses, lost income, pain and suffering, and the clarity of liability. Minor injury claims (soft tissue, no hospitalization) settle for $5,000–$30,000. Moderate injury claims (fractures, surgery) range $35,000–$120,000. Serious injury claims (permanent disability, traumatic brain injury, wrongful death) reach $200,000–$2M+. Baltimore City juries are known for plaintiff-friendly verdicts, often exceeding settlement offers by 2–3x in cases that go to trial.

Maryland's Contributory Negligence Rule — A Trap for the Unwary

Maryland is one of only four states that still follows pure contributory negligence. If you are found even 1% at fault for the accident, you recover nothing — zero. This harsh rule makes evidence preservation critical. Never speak to the other driver's insurance company without an attorney. A skilled Baltimore injury lawyer builds a case that leaves no room for the defense to assign you any blame, by securing witness statements, traffic camera footage, and expert accident reconstruction immediately.

Maryland's Statute of Limitations

Maryland gives you just 3 years from the accident date to file a personal injury lawsuit. For claims against government entities (city buses, state roads, police vehicles), you have only 180 days to file a notice of claim. Miss it and your case is dismissed permanently. An attorney files a protective lawsuit before the deadline while continuing to negotiate a settlement with the insurance company.
