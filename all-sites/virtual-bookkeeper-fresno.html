How Much Does Virtual Bookkeeping Cost in Fresno?

Virtual bookkeeping for Fresno small businesses runs $200–$500/month for basic monthly bookkeeping (bank reconciliation, expense categorization, monthly P&L), $400–$800/month with payroll processing, and $600–$1,200/month for full-service including sales tax filing and quarterly estimated tax support. Setup fees run $300–$1,000 depending on how messy the prior books are.

Compare that to an in-office bookkeeper in Fresno at $25–$45/hour — a typical 15-employee business needs 15–25 hours of bookkeeping a month, which pencils out to $500–$1,000/month before payroll, payroll taxes, and benefits. Virtual bookkeepers using QuickBooks Online or Xero typically deliver the same work for less because they have no office overhead, and the books are tax-ready at year end — which saves $500–$2,000 in CPA cleanup fees.

Why Fresno Businesses Need a Bookkeeper Who Knows California

California's compliance load is heavy even for a one-person shop. Every business that pays employees must handle state disability insurance (SDI), unemployment insurance (EUTF), and paid sick leave, and employers with 5+ employees must offer retirement access through CalSavers. Fresno's agricultural and logistics economy adds industry-specific rules: seasonal payroll timing for farm labor, IFTA fuel-tax filings for trucking, and sales-tax registration with CDTFA for anyone selling goods.

Missed filings trigger automatic penalties: EDD late filings start around $250 per return, FTB late franchise tax accrues 5% per month up to 25%, and CDTFA sales tax penalties can hit 10% plus interest. A virtual bookkeeper who files California payroll and sales tax returns monthly keeps you out of that penalty spiral — most Fresno businesses hire one after their first EDD or FTB penalty notice.

Monthly Bookkeeping: What You Should Get for the Money

A proper monthly bookkeeping package includes: bank and credit card reconciliation, expense categorization, accounts receivable/payable tracking, a monthly profit-and-loss statement and balance sheet, and a clean handoff to your CPA at tax time. You should also get a monthly review call — a bookkeeper who only sends a dashboard without explaining the numbers isn't doing the job.

Catch-up bookkeeping is the most common first engagement: Fresno businesses routinely show up with 6–18 months of unreconciled accounts because the owner "was too busy" — and the cost of untangling it is $500–$2,500 depending on volume. The fix is usually switching to a monthly subscription after the cleanup, which keeps the books current and makes tax season a 30-minute meeting instead of a panic.

Payroll and Sales Tax for Fresno Businesses

Fresno's combined sales tax rate is roughly 8.35% — California's 7.25% base plus Fresno County's 0.25% and local transportation measures. If you sell taxable goods or services, CDTFA registration is mandatory, and returns are due monthly, quarterly, or annually depending on volume. Filing late accrues a 10% penalty plus interest automatically.

Payroll runs on a tight schedule: federal deposits due the day after payday for large employers, EDD quarterly returns (DE 9/DE 9C) due the last day of April, July, October, and January, and year-end W-2s by January 31. A virtual bookkeeper with payroll handles the full cycle — pay runs, EDD filings, 941s, and W-2s — for $50–$150 per payroll run on top of the monthly fee. For a 10-person Fresno company, that's a fraction of what an HR payroll service charges.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a virtual bookkeeper really handle a Fresno business remotely?

Yes — modern bookkeeping is done in QuickBooks Online or Xero, which both you and the bookkeeper access securely. You connect your bank feeds, send receipts by photo or email, and review everything on a monthly call. Physical location doesn't matter for the work.

What's the difference between a bookkeeper and a CPA?

A bookkeeper keeps your day-to-day records current — reconciliation, categorization, payroll, and monthly reports. A CPA handles tax strategy, prepares and files returns, and can represent you before the IRS and FTB. Most Fresno businesses use both: a virtual bookkeeper monthly, a CPA at year end.

How much does catch-up bookkeeping cost in Fresno?

$500–$2,500 depending on how many months of transactions and how many accounts need reconciling. Many virtual bookkeepers offer a fixed catch-up price, then transition you to a monthly subscription once the books are clean.

Do I need to file California sales tax if I only sell services?

Most services are not taxable in California, but some are (repair, fabrication, and certain digital products), and if you sell any physical goods, you're taxable. A bookkeeper can tell you whether your specific service line needs CDTFA registration — guessing wrong means penalties either way.

Will my books be CPA-ready at tax time?

Yes — that's the core deliverable. A good virtual bookkeeper hands your CPA a clean trial balance, reconciled accounts, and categorized transactions, which cuts tax prep time and fees. Ask any candidate for a sample of how they hand off to CPAs.
