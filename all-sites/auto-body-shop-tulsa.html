Collision Repair Costs in Tulsa

Minor dent and scratch repair runs $200–$750. Major collision work (bumper replacement, quarter panel work, frame straightening) ranges $1,200–$5,500 depending on parts and labor. Tulsa labor rates average $50–$70 per hour for body work and $80–$110 for structural frame repair. Most shops provide free written estimates and work directly with your insurance adjuster. Paintless dent repair (PDR) for hail damage — common in Oklahoma's storm season — runs $75–$150 per panel and doesn't require repainting.

Oklahoma Insurance — What Collision Coverage Pays

Oklahoma is an at-fault state. If another driver caused the accident, their property damage liability covers your repairs. If you're at fault or the other driver is uninsured (Oklahoma has one of the highest uninsured driver rates in the US at 14%), your collision coverage pays minus your deductible. Important: Oklahoma does NOT require insurers to pay for OEM parts on vehicles over 2 years old — they can insist on aftermarket or used parts. A good body shop documents the difference and argues for OEM when safety-critical components are involved.

Paint Matching in Oklahoma's Sun

Oklahoma's intense sun (220+ sunny days per year) fades car paint faster than national averages. A proper paint job uses a spectrophotometer to read your car's current color (not the factory code), accounts for UV fade, and blends into adjacent panels. Shops that skip blending or rely on generic color codes produce visible mismatches that become obvious in Tulsa's direct sunlight. Always insist on computerized color matching with a blend-to-adjacent-panel guarantee in writing.
