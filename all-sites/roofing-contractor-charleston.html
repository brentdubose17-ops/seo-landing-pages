Licensed Roofing Contractors in Charleston, SC

Protecting a Charleston home from coastal Atlantic storms, hurricane-force winds, and blistering salt air demands expert roofing craftsmanship. Our licensed South Carolina roofing contractors specialize in storm damage repair, FORTIFIED Roof™ installations, historical standing seam metal, and architectural shingle replacements across the Lowcountry.

Lowcountry Weather & Coastal Roof Durability

Charleston's unique coastal environment subjects roofs to severe thermal expansion, salt-air corrosion, and intense tropical storm winds exceeding 110 mph. High humidity and heavy rainfall make roofs vulnerable to algae growth, flashing leaks around chimneys, and hidden sub-roof rot in historic Downtown homes and suburban properties in Mount Pleasant, West Ashley, and James Island.

We install wind-resistant architectural asphalt shingles rated up to 130 mph, Class 4 impact-resistant shingles, and heavy-gauge standing seam aluminum or copper roofs designed specifically to withstand harsh marine climates without rusting or lifting.

SC Building Codes & Charleston Historic Guidelines

Roofing projects in the City of Charleston must conform to the 2021 South Carolina Building Code and local wind-borne debris zone regulations. Properties located within historic district overlay zones must also satisfy Board of Architectural Review (BAR) material and aesthetic guidelines.

South Carolina homeowners can take advantage of the SC Safe Home grant program, which offers matching grants up to $5,000 to fortify roof structures against windstorm damage. Upgrading to wind-rated roofing can also reduce annual homeowner insurance premiums by 15% to 30%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What wind rating is required for roofs in Charleston, SC?

Charleston's coastal location requires roofing materials rated for wind speeds between 130 mph and 140 mph depending on proximity to open water.

Does SC Safe Home provide grants for roof replacement?

Yes, eligible South Carolina homeowners can receive up to $5,000 in matching grants to fortify their roof deck against hurricane wind damage.

Do historic homes in Downtown Charleston need BAR approval for roofing?

Yes, properties within the BAR jurisdiction must receive historic review approval for material, color, and roof profile alterations.

How long does a roof replacement take in Charleston?

Most residential roof replacements in the Lowcountry are completed within 1 to 2 days, including complete tear-off and debris cleanup.

What metal roofing is best for coastal Charleston homes?

Standing seam aluminum or marine-grade copper is recommended over galvanized steel due to superior resistance to salt-air corrosion.
