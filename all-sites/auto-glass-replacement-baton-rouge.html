Louisiana Vehicle Inspection & Insurance Rules

Under Louisiana Revised Statute (LA RS 32:358), driving with a cracked windshield that obscures the driver's vision or interferes with windshield wiper operation is illegal and results in a failed state vehicle inspection sticker.

Louisiana law allows vehicle owners to select their preferred auto glass provider for insurance claims. Most comprehensive auto insurance policies cover rock chip repairs at 100% with zero out-of-pocket deductible.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a windshield chip be repaired instead of replaced?

Chips smaller than a quarter and cracks under 6 inches can usually be repaired with optical resin if they are not directly in the driver's line of sight.

Does mobile windshield service cost extra in Baton Rouge?

No, mobile technician service to your home or office is included at no extra charge across East Baton Rouge Parish.

How long do I need to wait to drive after windshield replacement?

Our fast-curing adhesive allows safe driving within 60 minutes after installation is complete.

Will my insurance cover windshield replacement in Louisiana?

Comprehensive insurance covers glass damage, and rock chip repairs are typically fully covered with $0 deductible.

Why is ADAS recalibration necessary after windshield installation?

Forward-facing safety cameras mounted on the windshield must be calibrated precisely so lane keeping and auto-braking function accurately.
