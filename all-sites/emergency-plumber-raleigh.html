What Licenses Do Plumbers Need in North Carolina?

North Carolina plumbers are licensed by the NC State Board of Plumbing Examiners, with separate classifications for residential and commercial work. The City of Raleigh (Development Services) requires plumbing permits for water heater replacements, repipes, and most fixture work — the permit is usually included in the contractor's quote.

Unlicensed "handyman" plumbing is a common insurance trap in Raleigh's fast-growing market. If a handyman's unpermitted work causes a leak, your homeowner's insurance can deny the claim. Always verify the license number on the Board's website and ask whether the job includes a permit.

Why Do Pipes Burst in Raleigh Winters?

Raleigh winters are a freeze-thaw roller coaster: nights drop into the teens and 20s, then afternoons warm above freezing — exactly the cycle that stresses uninsulated pipes. Crawlspace and exterior-wall pipes are the most vulnerable, and January and February bring the bulk of burst-pipe calls across Wake County.

Prevention is cheap: insulate exposed pipes in the crawlspace ($30–$80 in materials), seal foundation vents before hard freezes, and let a faucet drip on nights below 25°F. Homes built before 1990 without conditioned crawlspaces are the highest risk — if you own one, an annual pre-winter plumbing inspection ($99–$150) is worth it.

Water Heater: Repair or Replace in Raleigh?

Tank water heaters last 10–12 years in Raleigh's moderately hard water. If yours is leaking from the tank itself, replacement is the only option — a 40-gallon tank runs $1,200–$2,800 installed, and a tankless unit runs $2,500–$5,000. A permit is required for both in Raleigh, so factor that into quotes.

If the unit isn't leaking, diagnose before replacing: a failed heating element ($150–$300 with labor), bad thermostat ($100–$250), or sediment buildup (flush: $150–$250) are all fixable. On an 8+ year-old tank with recurring issues, replacement beats a third repair — energy savings alone on a new high-efficiency unit run $150–$300 a year.
