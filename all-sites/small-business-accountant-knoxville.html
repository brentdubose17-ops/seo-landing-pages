Tennessee Tax Advantages for Knoxville Businesses

Tennessee has no state income tax, no capital gains tax, and no corporate income tax. That is a major advantage if you operate an S-corp or LLC and pay yourself dividends or distributions.

But don't mistake 'no income tax' for 'no taxes.' You still owe federal taxes, self-employment tax (if sole proprietor), payroll taxes (if you have employees), and sales tax on taxable goods/services.

Knoxville businesses should model S-corp vs. LLC elections. An S-corp elects to be taxed at the corporate level, then pays you a reasonable salary plus distributions. Sole proprietors pay 15.3% self-employment tax; S-corp owners pay only payroll tax on the salary portion (you avoid SE tax on distributions).

Sales Tax Compliance in Knox County, Tennessee

Tennessee requires a sales tax permit if you sell taxable goods (not services unless specifically listed). Knox County adds a local rate on top of the state rate.

Filing frequency is based on your sales volume: monthly if over $3,600/month, quarterly if under. Penalties for late filing are steep.

Collect sales tax from customers and remit to the state. If you forget to collect or under-remit, you are liable — the tax liability does not disappear.

Entity Selection: Sole Proprietor, LLC, or S-Corp

A sole proprietor has no liability protection and pays 15.3% self-employment tax on all net income. Simple to set up but riskier.

An LLC (Limited Liability Company) protects personal assets and is taxed as a partnership (pass-through to personal tax return). No self-employment tax savings over sole prop.

An S-corp (for tax purposes, not legal structure) pays payroll tax on a reasonable salary but avoids SE tax on distributions. Saves 15% on income over the salary threshold, but requires quarterly payroll filing.

For Knoxville businesses with $60k+ net income, an S-corp election usually pays for itself in tax savings.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an accountant cost for a Knoxville small business?

Annual tax returns cost $400–$800 for sole props, $600–$1,500 for LLCs/S-corps. Monthly bookkeeping is $150–$400. Payroll runs $5–$15 per employee per pay period.

Does Tennessee have income tax?

No state income tax, capital gains tax, or corporate income tax. But you still owe federal income tax, self-employment tax, payroll taxes, and sales tax if applicable.

Should my Knoxville business be an S-corp?

If you have $60k+ net income, an S-corp election typically saves 15% on the income above a reasonable salary. Model it against your specific situation with an accountant.

What is Knox County's sales tax rate?

Tennessee state rate is 9.55%; Knox County adds local components. Exact rate depends on the city. Check with the state before filing.

How often do I file sales tax in Knoxville?

Monthly if sales exceed $3,600/month, quarterly if below. Filing frequency is assigned by the Tennessee Department of Revenue based on your volume.
