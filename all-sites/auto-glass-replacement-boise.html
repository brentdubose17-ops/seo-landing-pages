Idaho Insurance Rules for Auto Glass

Idaho requires comprehensive coverage insurers to offer glass repair with no deductible for chip repairs. Full windshield replacement falls under comprehensive with your standard deductible ($100–$500). Some insurers (GEICO, Progressive, State Farm) offer $0 deductible glass endorsements for $2–$5 per month. Key difference: Idaho has one of the highest rates of uninsured drivers in the region (12%), so make sure your policy includes uninsured motorist property damage — it covers windshield damage caused by debris from other vehicles even if the driver can't be identified.
