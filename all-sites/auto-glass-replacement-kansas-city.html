Hail Damage to Windows in Kansas City

Kansas City hail season (April–June) creates massive demand for glass replacement. Hail pits the windshield and can crack windows. Most damage is covered by comprehensive insurance.

Pit damage alone won't require replacement if the pits don't compromise visibility or the windshield integrity. But if the windshield cracks, replacement is necessary immediately — a crack spreads fast.

Document hail damage with photos for your insurance claim. File within 30 days to ensure coverage.

ADAS Calibration on Modern Kansas City Vehicles

Vehicles from 2018 onward often have camera-based driver assistance systems (ADAS): lane keeping, automatic braking, adaptive cruise. The windshield camera must be recalibrated after windshield replacement.

ADAS recalibration costs $150–$400 and is critical for safety. Some glass shops include it; others charge extra. Confirm before you book.

If you skip recalibration, lane-keeping and braking features may malfunction. Insurance doesn't typically cover ADAS recalibration separately, so get it with your glass replacement.

Insurance Coverage for Glass in Kansas City

Comprehensive insurance covers glass damage from hail, rocks, vandalism, and theft. Collision does not cover glass. Most policies allow you to choose any glass shop, but using an insurer-preferred provider often waives your deductible.

Glass-only insurance riders are available through some insurers and lower your deductible to $0 for glass damage. Cost is usually $20–$50 per year — worthwhile in a hail zone.

Call your insurer before getting replacement and confirm coverage. Get a quote from the glass shop and send it to the insurer for approval.
