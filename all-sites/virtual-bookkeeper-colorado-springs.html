Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a virtual bookkeeper cost in Colorado Springs?

Monthly virtual bookkeeping: $200–$600. Hourly catch-up projects: $50–$100/hr. QuickBooks migration: $500–$1,500 flat fee. Virtual is typically 25–40% cheaper than in-person bookkeeping.

Is my financial data secure with a virtual bookkeeper?

Professional virtual bookkeepers use encrypted cloud platforms (QuickBooks Online, Xero) with bank-level encryption, multi-factor authentication, and role-based access controls. Never share direct login credentials — use the platform's user-invite feature.

Do I need a local bookkeeper or remote is fine?

Remote is fine for most Colorado Springs businesses. Virtual bookkeepers handle Colorado-specific sales tax, personal property tax, and Secretary of State filings through online portals. In-person bookkeeping is only needed if you regularly hand over physical receipts and paperwork.

How quickly can I get started with a virtual bookkeeper?

Most virtual bookkeepers can onboard new clients within 1 week. You'll need QuickBooks Online access (or they set it up for you), bank feed connections, and a 1-hour kickoff call to review your chart of accounts and processes.

Can a virtual bookkeeper help with tax preparation?

Yes, though many virtual bookkeepers prepare your books for your CPA's tax filing rather than filing taxes themselves. Some offer bundled bookkeeping + tax preparation services. Clarify this during your consultation.
