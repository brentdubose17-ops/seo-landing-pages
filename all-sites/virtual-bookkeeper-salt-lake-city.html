Utah Sales Tax and Business Filing Requirements

Utah imposes a 4.85% state sales tax with local options that make the combined rate in Salt Lake City 7.25%. All businesses collecting sales tax must register with the Utah State Tax Commission and file electronically — monthly, quarterly, or annually depending on volume. Utah also requires a $15 annual business license renewal through the SLC Mayor's Office of Economic Development. Late sales tax filings incur a 10% penalty plus 0.5% monthly interest, so accurate timely bookkeeping is essential.
