Florida Sales Tax and Compliance: The Tampa Angle

Florida has no state income tax — a huge advantage for business owners — but sales tax compliance is strict. Florida's base rate is 6%, and Hillsborough County adds a 1% discretionary surtax (7% total in Tampa). A virtual bookkeeper who knows Florida handles: monthly/quarterly sales tax returns through the Florida Department of Revenue portal, reemployment tax (unemployment) filings, and the $138.75 annual LLC report to the FL Division of Corporations.

Many Tampa businesses miss the tangible personal property tax return (due April 1 each year via the Property Appraiser) — a virtual bookkeeper tracks it so you don't pay penalties plus interest (up to 25% of the tax due). FL DOR assessments for late sales tax run 10% of the tax due plus 1.5% monthly interest, so filing on time matters more than in most states.

Can a virtual bookkeeper handle my Florida sales tax?

Yes — a Florida-experienced virtual bookkeeper files your sales tax returns through the FL DOR portal, tracks the 7% Hillsborough County rate, and handles tangible personal property tax filings.

What if my Tampa business books are a mess?

Is my financial data safe with a virtual bookkeeper?

Do I need a Tampa-based bookkeeper or is remote fine?

Remote is fine for 95% of Tampa businesses — Florida has no state income tax, and the filings (sales tax, annual report) are all online. You gain cost savings and hurricane-resilience by going virtual.
