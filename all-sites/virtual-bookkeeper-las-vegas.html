Nevada Tax Advantages for Small Businesses

Nevada is one of the most tax-friendly states for businesses. There is no state income tax, no corporate income tax, no franchise tax, and no personal income tax. This alone can save Nevada businesses 5–10% compared to California or New York. However, Nevada does impose specific taxes that require careful bookkeeping:

  • Modified Business Tax (MBT): 0.47% on wages exceeding $50,000 per quarter for general businesses. Financial institutions pay a higher rate (2%). Due quarterly.
  • Commerce Tax: Applies only if gross revenue exceeds $4 million annually. Rates vary by industry (0.051% to 0.331%).
  • Clark County sales tax: Las Vegas combined rate is approximately 8.375% (state 4.6% + county 2.25% + transportation 0.5% + stadium 0.88% + infrastructure 0.145%).
  • Business license: All Nevada businesses must register with the Secretary of State ($200–$500 initial, $200 annual renewal).

Core services include: categorizing all income and expenses, reconciling bank and credit card accounts monthly, managing accounts payable (bill pay) and accounts receivable (invoicing), preparing monthly financial statements (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow), tracking 1099 contractor payments, filing quarterly sales tax returns with the Nevada Department of Taxation, and preparing year-end financial packages for your CPA or tax preparer.

Many Las Vegas businesses in hospitality, construction, and healthcare have unique bookkeeping needs — tip reporting, prevailing wage tracking, patient billing — that a virtual bookkeeper familiar with Nevada regulations can handle correctly. The goal is to give you accurate, real-time financial data so you can make informed decisions without spending your evenings on QuickBooks.
