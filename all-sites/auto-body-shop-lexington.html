Kentucky Auto Repair Laws & Insurance Freedom

Under Kentucky insurance laws (KRS 304.20-060), vehicle owners have the legal right to choose any licensed auto body repair shop. Insurance companies cannot dictate where your vehicle is repaired or force the use of non-OEM aftermarket parts without your explicit consent.

We work directly with all major insurance carriers—including State Farm, GEICO, Progressive, and Allstate—handling claims paperwork, supplemental appraisals, and rental car arrangements to make the repair process seamless.

Frequently Asked Questions

Am I required to use the body shop suggested by my insurance company in Kentucky?

No, Kentucky law guarantees your right to choose any licensed auto body shop for collision repairs.

What is Paintless Dent Repair (PDR) and does it work for hail damage?

PDR uses specialized tools to gently flex metal back into position without disturbing factory paint, preserving original finish and resale value.

Do you calibrate ADAS sensors after bumper or windshield repair?

Yes, modern safety systems like radar sensors and lane departure cameras are recalibrated to factory tolerances after body work.

How long do typical collision repairs take in Lexington?

Minor dent or bumper repairs take 2 to 3 days, while major structural collision repairs typically require 5 to 10 business days.

Do collision repairs come with a warranty?

Yes, our body shop provides a lifetime warranty on all paintwork and structural workmanship for as long as you own the vehicle.
