Roofing Contractor in Raleigh, NC

Last updated: July 2026 · Serving Raleigh and surrounding areas

Your roof is your home's first defense against Raleigh's increasingly severe thunderstorms, hail, and hurricane remnants. Whether you need a full replacement after storm damage or a repair to stop an active leak, choosing the right roofing contractor in Raleigh makes the difference between a 30-year roof and a 10-year headache.

How Much Does a New Roof Cost in Raleigh?

Raleigh homeowners typically pay $6,500–$14,000 for a standard asphalt shingle roof (30-year architectural) on a 2,000 sq ft home. Metal roofing runs $12,000–$25,000 but lasts 40–70 years. Storm damage claims through insurance cover most or all of the cost if the damage exceeds your deductible — a licensed roofer helps you document it properly.

Storm Season in the Triangle — What to Watch For

Raleigh averages 40–50 thunderstorms per year plus the tail end of Atlantic hurricane season (June–November). After any storm with winds over 50 mph, inspect for: missing or curled shingles, granule loss in gutters (looks like black sand), flashing separation around chimneys and vents, and interior ceiling stains that may not appear until 2–3 rain cycles later. Most Raleigh roofers offer free storm damage inspections — take advantage of them.

Metal vs. Shingle Roofs for Raleigh Homes

Architectural asphalt shingles ($3.50–$5.50/sq ft installed) dominate the Triangle market because they're affordable and suit HOAs. Standing-seam metal ($8–$16/sq ft) costs more upfront but handles Raleigh's hail better, reflects solar heat (reducing AC costs 10–25%), and carries a 40+ year lifespan. If you plan to stay in your home 10+ years, metal's lifetime cost is often lower.
