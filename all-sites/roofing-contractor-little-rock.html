Arkansas Storm Damage — What Insurance Covers

Arkansas ranks among the top 10 states for hail claims, and Little Rock sits squarely in Tornado Alley. Wind and hail damage are covered under standard homeowners policies. If your roof is under 10 years old and has significant hail damage, most insurers cover full replacement minus your deductible ($1,000–$2,500 typical). The key: get a roofing inspection within 12 months of a known storm event — Arkansas law allows claims up to 1 year after the storm, and insurance companies use the storm date, not the inspection date, to determine coverage. Many Little Rock homeowners miss this window and end up paying out of pocket.

Little Rock's Climate — What It Means for Your Roof

Little Rock experiences over 50 inches of annual rainfall and an average of 60+ thunderstorm days per year. This combination of heat, humidity, and violent storms accelerates roof deterioration. Asphalt shingles in Little Rock typically last 18–22 years (vs. 25–30 in drier climates). Moss and algae growth is a persistent problem — zinc strips or copper-infused shingles prevent this. After every major storm, inspect for loose or missing shingles, granule loss in gutters, and flashing damage around chimneys and vents.
