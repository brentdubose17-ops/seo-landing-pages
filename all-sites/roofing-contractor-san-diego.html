How Much Does Roofing Cost in San Diego?

San Diego roof repairs range from $400–$1,800 depending on the extent of damage and roof type. Full replacements: asphalt shingle $7,000–$14,000, concrete tile $15,000–$30,000, clay tile $20,000–$40,000, and flat roofs (TPO or modified bitumen) $6,000–$12,000. Permit fees in San Diego add $400–$1,200 for a full replacement. Most contractors offer financing at 0–6% APR for qualified homeowners.

San Diego's Unique Roofing Challenges

San Diego's Mediterranean climate creates specific roofing issues: UV degradation from 260+ days of sun per year, Santa Ana wind damage that lifts tiles and shingles, and termite infestation in wood shake roofs (especially in canyons and coastal areas). Coastal homes within 5 miles of the ocean also face salt-air corrosion on metal flashings and fasteners. Concrete and clay tile last 40–50 years here, while asphalt shingles need replacement every 15–20 years due to sun exposure.

California Roofing Contractor Licensing

California requires roofing contractors to hold a C-39 Roofing Contractor license from the CSLB. This requires 4 years of journey-level experience, passing the trade exam and business law exam, and carrying liability insurance of at least $1M. San Diego County also requires city business licenses in incorporated cities like Chula Vista, Oceanside, and Escondido. Verify the CSLB license number and ask for proof of workers' comp insurance before signing a contract.
