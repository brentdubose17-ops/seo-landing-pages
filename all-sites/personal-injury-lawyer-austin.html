How Much Does a Personal Injury Lawyer Cost in Austin?

Austin personal injury lawyers almost exclusively work on contingency — meaning you pay nothing upfront. The attorney takes a percentage of the final settlement or verdict, typically 33% for pre-litigation settlements, 40% if a lawsuit is filed, and 40–45% if the case goes to trial. If the case doesn't result in a recovery, you owe nothing.

Most Austin firms also cover case expenses (medical record retrieval, expert witness fees, court filing costs) upfront and recover them from the settlement. This means you can pursue justice regardless of your financial situation. Free initial consultations are universal — you'll meet with the attorney, discuss your case, and get an honest assessment of its value and likelihood of success with no obligation.

Texas Statute of Limitations — Don't Wait

Texas law gives you just 2 years from the date of injury to file a personal injury lawsuit (Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code § 16.003). This deadline is strict — courts rarely grant extensions. The clock starts ticking on the day of the accident, not when you first feel symptoms or receive a diagnosis.

There are limited exceptions: the deadline may be tolled (paused) for minors, mentally incapacitated individuals, or if the defendant leaves the state. However, these exceptions are narrow and require immediate legal guidance. Filing even one day late means your case is permanently dismissed, regardless of its merits. Contact an Austin personal injury lawyer as soon as possible after any accident.

What Is My Austin Personal Injury Case Worth?

Settlement values in Austin vary widely based on case specifics. Minor accidents with soft tissue injuries (whiplash, bruising) typically settle for $5,000–$25,000. Moderate injuries with documented medical treatment and some lost wages range $25,000–$100,000. Severe injuries involving surgery, permanent impairment, or significant disfigurement command $100,000–$500,000+.

Catastrophic injury cases — traumatic brain injury, spinal cord damage, amputation, wrongful death — can reach $500,000 to multi-millions. Settlement calculation factors include: total medical expenses (past and future), lost income and reduced earning capacity, pain and suffering, property damage, and the clarity of liability. Texas's modified comparative fault rule (51% bar) means you can recover only if you're less than 51% at fault, and your recovery is reduced by your percentage of fault.

Steps to Take After an Austin Car Accident

  1. Ensure safety: Move to a safe location if possible. Call 911 for injuries.
  2. Document everything: Take photos of all vehicles, the scene, injuries, road conditions, and license plates.
  3. Exchange information: Insurance details, names, phone numbers, and witness contact info.
  4. Seek medical attention: Even if you feel fine — adrenaline masks injury symptoms. Some injuries (herniated discs, concussions) take days to appear.
  5. Notify your insurer: Report the accident promptly but stick to facts. Do not admit fault.
  6. Contact a lawyer before giving a recorded statement to the other party's insurance company. Their goal is to minimize payout. An attorney protects your rights.
