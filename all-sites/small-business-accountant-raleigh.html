What Does a Small Business Accountant Cost in Raleigh?

Monthly bookkeeping runs $300–$800 depending on transaction volume. Tax preparation for an LLC or S-Corp: $800–$2,500 including federal and state returns. Quarterly estimated tax calculation and filing: $150–$350 per quarter. Many Raleigh accountants offer flat-fee packages combining bookkeeping + tax prep for $500–$1,200/month — less than the cost of one IRS penalty.

North Carolina Business Taxes You Need to Know

North Carolina imposes a flat 2.5% corporate income tax on C-corps (falling to 0% by 2030). LLCs and S-corps pass through to personal income (4.75%–7.00% brackets). All businesses collecting sales tax must file electronically — the NC DOR requires monthly or quarterly filings depending on volume. Late sales tax filings carry a 5% penalty plus 0.5% monthly interest.

When to Hire an Accountant vs. DIY Software

If your business has under $50K revenue, no employees, and simple finances, QuickBooks or Wave may suffice. Hire a CPA when your revenue exceeds $100K, you have employees, you're forming an S-Corp or multi-member LLC, or you received an IRS or NC DOR notice. The cost of fixing a DIY mistake — amended returns, penalty abatement letters, audit representation — almost always exceeds a year of professional accounting fees.
