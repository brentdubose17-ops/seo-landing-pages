Arlington's Construction and Road Debris Hazard

Arlington's rapid growth means constant road construction along I-30, 360, and Matlock Road. Construction zones produce loose gravel, dropped tools, and tire debris that cause the majority of windshield damage in the city. The summer heat (100°F+) also makes existing chips expand into full cracks — what starts as a dime-sized chip can become a windshield-length crack within one 100-degree day. That's why Arlington auto glass shops emphasize that small chip repairs cost $50–$120 while procrastination leads to $300+ full replacements.

Texas Windshield Insurance Laws

Texas is one of the best states for windshield coverage. State law requires auto insurers offering comprehensive coverage to include glass repair with no deductible. For full replacement, most comprehensive policies apply the standard deductible, but many insurers offer $0 deductible glass add-ons for just a few dollars per month. Unlike some states, Texas has no restriction on aftermarket glass, but reputable shops always offer OEM-equivalent glass that meets federal safety standards for impact resistance and optical clarity.
