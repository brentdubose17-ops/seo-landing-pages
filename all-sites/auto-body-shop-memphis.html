Paintless Dent Repair (PDR) vs. Traditional Repair

Paintless Dent Repair (PDR) is the fastest and cheapest option for hail damage and minor dents. A technician uses specialized tools to massage the dent out from behind the panel without breaking the paint. Cost is $150–$400 per panel.

Traditional repair involves removing the panel, straightening it, and repainting. This costs $300–$800 per panel and takes longer but is necessary if the paint is cracked or the dent is too large for PDR.

For hail damage, PDR is usually the first choice if the paint is intact. If multiple panels are dented, the labor savings add up fast.

Working with Insurance on Your Auto Body Claim

When you file a collision or hail claim, provide photos and get a repair estimate from the body shop. The insurer sends an adjuster to inspect and approve the estimate (or counter-offer a lower amount).

Many body shops work directly with insurers and handle the estimate negotiation. You typically only pay your deductible ($250–$1,000) and the shop bills the insurer for the rest.

Request that the shop repair any mechanical issues discovered during the repair work. Some shops include this; others charge separately.

Right-to-Choose-Shop in Tennessee

Tennessee law allows you to choose any repair shop, not just the insurer's network. The insurer must pay for repairs at your chosen shop if the repair meets quality and safety standards.

Don't let the insurance company force you to use a chain shop. A local body shop often provides better quality and faster service than a high-volume franchise.
