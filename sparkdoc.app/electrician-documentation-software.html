Why Pre-Work Documentation Matters More Than Ever

In the electrical trade, the difference between a profitable project and a costly dispute often comes down to what you can prove. A single change order that wasn’t documented, a wall you opened that looked different from the blueprint, or a missing photo of existing wiring can lead to chargebacks, delays, or even legal trouble. Modern electrician documentation software solves this by making photo logs, time-stamped notes, and digital signatures part of your daily workflow — not an afterthought.
