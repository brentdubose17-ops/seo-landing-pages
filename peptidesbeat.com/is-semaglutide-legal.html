Is Semaglutide Legal? Current Status & Regulatory Clarity

Is semaglutide legal? This is one of the most frequent questions we hear from clinics, researchers, and patients navigating the evolving peptide landscape. As of the latest FDA updates, semaglutide remains a prescription-only medication approved for type 2 diabetes and weight management (under brand names Ozempic, Wegovy, Rybelsus). However, its regulatory status is dynamic — especially as the FDA reclassifies certain peptides and issues enforcement discretion periods. Is Semaglutide Legal for Research and Compounding?

For researchers and compounding pharmacies, the answer depends on sourcing and intended use.
