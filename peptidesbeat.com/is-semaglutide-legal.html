Current legal status of semaglutide

Semaglutide is FDA-approved under brand names Ozempic®, Wegovy®, and Rybelsus® for specific indications. These products are Schedule III? No — semaglutide is not a controlled substance at the federal level. However, its legal use depends on:

  • Prescription by a licensed practitioner for an approved indication (or off-label within accepted medical practice).
  • Dispensing from a registered pharmacy (including compounding pharmacies, under FD&C Act Section 503A/503B).
  • Research use under an IND or exempt preclinical protocols.

No federal ban exists, but the FDA has issued warnings about counterfeit or untested semaglutide from unregulated sources. For clinics, legality also hinges on state pharmacy boards and compounding regulations.
