Is semaglutide legal? Regulatory status & FDA clarity

Semaglutide — the GLP-1 receptor agonist widely used for type 2 diabetes and weight management — sits in a complex regulatory space. The short answer: semaglutide is legal when prescribed and dispensed within approved channels (brand names Ozempic®, Wegovy®, Rybelsus®). However, compounded or research-grade semaglutide occupies a gray zone following recent FDA reclassification of certain peptides. Is semaglutide legal under current FDA reclassification?

Yes, semaglutide remains a lawful prescription medicine when obtained through standard FDA-approved supply chains. In 2024, the FDA updated its bulk drug substances list for compounding, which directly affects how pharmacies can prepare semaglutide. While the drug itself is not scheduled as a controlled substance, any unapproved manufacturing or distribution (including certain compounded versions) may fall outside legal boundaries.
