Current Legal Status of Semaglutide

Semaglutide is legal in the United States when prescribed by a licensed healthcare provider for FDA-approved indications—specifically, for type 2 diabetes (under brands like Ozempic and Rybelsus) and for chronic weight management (under Wegovy). It is not a controlled substance and has not been scheduled by the DEA. However, its use must comply with federal and state prescribing laws. Unauthorized compounding or distribution of semaglutide outside of approved channels may raise legal concerns, particularly if it involves unapproved formulations or misleading claims.
